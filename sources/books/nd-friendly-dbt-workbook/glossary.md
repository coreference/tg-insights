# Glossary — The Neurodivergent Friendly Workbook of DBT Skills

Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **AAC** — a communication option listed alongside written communication as a meltdown prevention tip (the acronym is not spelled out in the book) [passage #44] · [Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/managing-meltdowns.md)
- **accessible mindfulness exercises** — mindfulness practices adapted for people who find visualising, breathing-based practice or focusing on internal sensations difficult [passage #32] · [Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation](topics/nd-mindfulness/accessible-mindfulness-exercises.md)
- **accommodations** — adjustments in an area of life that a person may need; the book spells it "accomodations" in places [passage #10, #11] · [Self-care, a guide to my needs and the support and accommodations wheel](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/self-care-needs-and-support-wheel.md)
- **Act Intentionally** — the author's renaming of Act Opposite: exploring how to respond intentionally to your emotions [passage #67] · [Check the Facts and Act Intentionally (neurodivergent-friendly Opposite Action)](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/check-facts-act-intentionally.md)
- **Act Opposite** — standard DBT skill: managing emotions by responding in an opposite way [passage #67] · [Check the Facts and Act Intentionally (neurodivergent-friendly Opposite Action)](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/check-facts-act-intentionally.md)
- **affirmation** — a short self-statement meant to bring comfort and counter negative internalised messages [passage #15, #16, #17] · [Affirmations for neurodivergent people and for rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD)](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-and-rsd-affirmations.md)
- **Alexithymia** — a difference in emotional processing in which a person struggles to identify, describe and express emotions; common in Autistic and ADHD people [passage #58, #64] · [Alexithymia and describing your emotions](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/alexithymia-describing-emotions.md); [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
- **aphantasia** — an inability to visualise something in one's mind; may be experienced by some neurodivergent, especially autistic, people [passage #19] · [Mindfulness adapted: attention, interoception and aphantasia differences, when to use it, and reflection](topics/nd-mindfulness/adapted-mindfulness-basics.md)
- **Check the facts** — DBT skill, here reframed as checking whether your response to an emotion fits the facts and is helpful [passage #65] · [Check the Facts and Act Intentionally (neurodivergent-friendly Opposite Action)](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/check-facts-act-intentionally.md)
- **Chewellery** — chewable jewellery, listed as a safe-space item [passage #14, #51, #73, #75] · [Building a sensory toolkit and a sensory-friendly safe space](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-toolkit-and-safe-space.md); [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md); [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **Compare Two** — an exercise of noticing the differences between two similar objects [passage #33] · [Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation](topics/nd-mindfulness/accessible-mindfulness-exercises.md)
- **Dialectical Behavioural Therapy (DBT)** — a therapy founded by Dr Marsha Linehan for people living with Borderline Personality Disorder; its skills help identify and regulate emotions, manage distress and recognise, fulfil and advocate for needs [passage #4] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md)
- **distress tolerance** — skills and tools to manage moments of distress, overwhelm and overload [passage #5, #39] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md); [Distress tolerance basics: the STOP skill and a personal crisis plan](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/stop-skill-and-crisis-plan.md)
- **dysregulated / overstimulated** — states (from stress, sensory input, emotions) that lead to meltdowns when a limit is reached and surpassed [passage #43] · [Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/managing-meltdowns.md)
- **Emotion wheel** — chart that helps identify and label emotions, especially complex ones [passage #58] · [Alexithymia and describing your emotions](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/alexithymia-describing-emotions.md)
- **emotional dysregulation** — the inability to regulate or control emotions, including emotional experiences, actions and responses [passage #64] · [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
- **emotional headache** — the author's term for how they feel after a meltdown [passage #43] · [Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/managing-meltdowns.md)
- **emotional intensity** — a self-rated 1–10 scale used to pick which skill area to try [passage #5] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md)
- **Emotional mind** — DBT state of mind in which emotions influence behaviour and thoughts; logic is hard to access [passage #21, #22] · [Wise Mind: balancing logical mind and emotional mind](topics/nd-mindfulness/wise-mind.md)
- **emotional regulation** — the ability to control and manage your emotions and your reactions to them, including when you have them, how you experience them and how you express or act on them [passage #5, #64] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md); [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
- **Emotional triangle** — the three things emotions can impact — behaviour (actions and urges), body (physiological reactions) and mind (thoughts and memories) [passage #56] · [Understanding emotions: their role, the emotional triangle and emotional responses](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/understanding-emotions.md)
- **External senses** — sight, touch, smell, taste and sound [passage #71] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **fight or flight** — the body's state during anxiety, panic or distress: racing heart, difficulty controlling breathing, odd feeling in the limbs [passage #40] · [Calming the body fast: mammalian diving reflex and the TIPP skill](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/diving-reflex-and-tipp.md)
- **freeing the stim** — letting yourself stim openly; the book calls it sometimes the perfect way to create harmony and calm [passage #34] · [Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation](topics/nd-mindfulness/accessible-mindfulness-exercises.md)
- **heavy work** — listed as a way to get sensory input ("do heavy work for input"; not defined further) [passage #30] · [Self check-in: noticing emotions, body, energy, breathing and focus, then acting on it](topics/nd-mindfulness/self-check-in.md)
- **hyperarousal** — being pushed out of the window because emotions get too much (e.g. anxiety, fast heart, racing thoughts) [passage #62] · [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
- **Hypersensitivity** — over-responsiveness to sensory input, which can cause sensory overload, distress, discomfort and pain [passage #71] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **hypoarousal** — being pushed out of the window because things get you down or you feel numb (e.g. dissociating, numbness, tiredness) [passage #62] · [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
- **Hyposensitivity** — under-responsiveness to sensory input; low levels of input are hard to register [passage #71] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **IMPROVE the environment** — the book's neurodivergent adaptation: changing the surroundings to tolerate emotions until their intensity drops [passage #49] · [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **IMPROVE the Moment** — standard DBT skill for tolerating emotions until they feel less intense by improving the moment [passage #49] · [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **Inner wisdom** — label the diagram attaches to wise mind [passage #22] · [Wise Mind: balancing logical mind and emotional mind](topics/nd-mindfulness/wise-mind.md)
- **Internal label maker** — the author's metaphor for what alexithymia lacks: an inner labeller for emotions [passage #58] · [Alexithymia and describing your emotions](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/alexithymia-describing-emotions.md)
- **Internal senses** — vestibular, proprioception and interoception [passage #71] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **interoception** — named as something some people already struggle with, making mindfulness harder (not defined on these pages) [passage #19, #71, #78] · [Mindfulness adapted: attention, interoception and aphantasia differences, when to use it, and reflection](topics/nd-mindfulness/adapted-mindfulness-basics.md); [Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-overload-and-profile.md); [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **interpersonal effectiveness** — one of the four original DBT components, intentionally removed in this workbook to keep it neurodivergent friendly [passage #4, #5] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md)
- **Logical mind** — DBT state of mind based on facts, reason and logic, in which emotions are not taken into consideration [passage #21, #22] · [Wise Mind: balancing logical mind and emotional mind](topics/nd-mindfulness/wise-mind.md)
- **mammalian diving reflex** — an innate physiological survival response that activates when submerged in freezing water; it activates the parasympathetic nervous system, slowing heart rate and breathing [passage #40] · [Calming the body fast: mammalian diving reflex and the TIPP skill](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/diving-reflex-and-tipp.md)
- **Masking** — hiding natural neurodivergent behaviour such as stimming [passage #51] · [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **meltdown** — an involuntary response that can occur in a time of crisis or distress, where we often lose control of our behaviour and responses [passage #43] · [Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/managing-meltdowns.md)
- **mindful stimming** — freeing the stim (e.g. stim dancing, fidget items) to focus, ground, regulate and come into the present [passage #34] · [Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation](topics/nd-mindfulness/accessible-mindfulness-exercises.md)
- **mindfulness** — the ability to be present in the moment, aware of what is happening within us and outside of us, without judgement; a foundational DBT skill [passage #5, #19] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md); [Mindfulness adapted: attention, interoception and aphantasia differences, when to use it, and reflection](topics/nd-mindfulness/adapted-mindfulness-basics.md)
- **multiply neurodivergent** — having more than one neurodivergence at once (the author: Autistic ADHDer living with Bipolar and in recovery with BPD) [passage #4] · [DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-adapted-dbt-and-skill-choice.md)
- **neurotypical standards** — rules and expectations designed for and by neurotypical people, which the affirmations reject as the measure for neurodivergent people [passage #15] · [Affirmations for neurodivergent people and for rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD)](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-and-rsd-affirmations.md)
- **Overstimulated** — the state of having taken in too much sensory input from environments that do not accommodate our needs [passage #76] · [Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-overload-and-profile.md)
- **parasympathetic nervous system** — the system the diving reflex activates; it "creates or mimics relaxation" [passage #40] · [Calming the body fast: mammalian diving reflex and the TIPP skill](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/diving-reflex-and-tipp.md)
- **personal anchor** — a physical object (personal or sensory) used instead of the breath as the focus that grounds you in the present [passage #35] · [Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation](topics/nd-mindfulness/accessible-mindfulness-exercises.md)
- **Personal crisis plan** — a pre-filled plan used as a reminder when you reach a point of distress [passage #13] · [Distress tolerance basics: the STOP skill and a personal crisis plan](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/stop-skill-and-crisis-plan.md)
- **Prompting event** — the trigger of an emotion, external or internal, possibly a combination of things [passage #57, #59] · [Alexithymia and describing your emotions](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/alexithymia-describing-emotions.md); [Understanding emotions: their role, the emotional triangle and emotional responses](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/understanding-emotions.md)
- **Proprioception** — glossed on the worksheet as external body awareness [passage #71, #78] · [Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-overload-and-profile.md); [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **Proprioceptive input** — deep pressure input (e.g. weighted blanket or pillow) that calms the nervous system [passage #50] · [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD)** — a sensitivity to rejection and criticism, common in neurodivergent people and especially ADHDers, causing an intense emotional response that reinforces messages that "we aren't good enough or no-one likes us" [passage #16] · [Affirmations for neurodivergent people and for rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD)](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/nd-and-rsd-affirmations.md)
- **RSD** — abbreviation the book uses for the condition behind "Rejection Sensitivity", named as a source of distress for neurodivergent people (not expanded on this page) [passage #39, #57] · [Distress tolerance basics: the STOP skill and a personal crisis plan](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/stop-skill-and-crisis-plan.md); [Understanding emotions: their role, the emotional triangle and emotional responses](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/understanding-emotions.md)
- **Secondary emotion** — an emotion triggered in response to the first emotion [passage #57] · [Understanding emotions: their role, the emotional triangle and emotional responses](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/understanding-emotions.md)
- **self check-in** — the skill of checking in with yourself to figure out your needs in the moment and respond to them [passage #29] · [Self check-in: noticing emotions, body, energy, breathing and focus, then acting on it](topics/nd-mindfulness/self-check-in.md)
- **self-care** — meeting our needs — physical, emotional, social and sensory — to look after well-being and prevent reaching points of distress [passage #7] · [Self-care, a guide to my needs and the support and accommodations wheel](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/self-care-needs-and-support-wheel.md)
- **Sensory diet** — listed as a hyposensitivity strategy for the vestibular sense; not defined in these pages [passage #75] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **sensory output** — listed as an action for low energy (not defined further) [passage #30] · [Self check-in: noticing emotions, body, energy, breathing and focus, then acting on it](topics/nd-mindfulness/self-check-in.md)
- **Sensory overload** — what happens when we receive too much input from our senses, more than the brain can process and handle [passage #76] · [Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-overload-and-profile.md)
- **Sensory processing system** — the system that takes in information from all the senses and processes it so we can respond [passage #71] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **Sensory profile** — one's own unique sensory processing system and needs [passage #71] · [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **Sensory safe space** — a space with sensory-friendly items one can escape to in times of need [passage #14] · [Building a sensory toolkit and a sensory-friendly safe space](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-toolkit-and-safe-space.md)
- **Sensory self-soothing** — distress tolerance skill: relieving distress or overwhelm by soothing yourself through the senses [passage #47] · [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **sensory toolbox** — a collection of your favourite sensory items, created as a prevention tip [passage #44] · [Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/managing-meltdowns.md)
- **Sensory toolkit** — a collection of items designed to soothe and comfort during distress or sensory overload [passage #79] · [Building a sensory toolkit and a sensory-friendly safe space](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-toolkit-and-safe-space.md)
- **Sensory tray** — listed as a safe-space item; not described further [passage #14] · [Building a sensory toolkit and a sensory-friendly safe space](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-toolkit-and-safe-space.md)
- **special interests** — listed as an emotional self-care need [passage #7] · [Self-care, a guide to my needs and the support and accommodations wheel](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/self-care-needs-and-support-wheel.md)
- **SpIns** — short for special interests [passage #52] · [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **spoons** — unit of energy used in the profile rating scale (level 5 = uses up all my spoons); not defined on these pages [passage #12, #77] · [Self-care, a guide to my needs and the support and accommodations wheel](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/self-care-needs-and-support-wheel.md); [Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-overload-and-profile.md)
- **stim dancing** — dancing as a form of stimming; suggested as a way to do the intense-exercise part of TIPP [passage #42] · [Calming the body fast: mammalian diving reflex and the TIPP skill](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/diving-reflex-and-tipp.md)
- **stimming** — self-stimulatory movements, sounds or sensations used to regulate emotions, sensations or thoughts [passage #7, #49, #51] · [Self-care, a guide to my needs and the support and accommodations wheel](topics/nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing/self-care-needs-and-support-wheel.md); [Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/self-soothing-improve-stims-spins.md)
- **STOP** — four-step skill: Stop pause and don't react; Take a step back; Observe; Proceed mindfully [passage #39] · [Distress tolerance basics: the STOP skill and a personal crisis plan](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/stop-skill-and-crisis-plan.md)
- **The middle path** — label the book's diagram gives to wise mind, between the cautious logical mind and the impulsive emotional mind [passage #22] · [Wise Mind: balancing logical mind and emotional mind](topics/nd-mindfulness/wise-mind.md)
- **TIPP** — DBT distress-tolerance acronym: Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Paired muscle relaxation [passage #42] · [Calming the body fast: mammalian diving reflex and the TIPP skill](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/diving-reflex-and-tipp.md)
- **TIPP skill** — distress tolerance skill listed as an option when scared or anxious (explained elsewhere in the book) [passage #68] · [Check the Facts and Act Intentionally (neurodivergent-friendly Opposite Action)](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/check-facts-act-intentionally.md)
- **unmasking** — listed as a prevention tip — allowing yourself space to unmask [passage #44] · [Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones](topics/nd-distress-tolerance/managing-meltdowns.md)
- **Value Action Plan** — worksheet pairing an identified value with actions that align with it [passage #27] · [Identifying your values and a value action plan](topics/nd-mindfulness/identify-values-action-plan.md)
- **values** — your morals, ethics, principles, or what you value in life and how you aspire to live; something that gives your life meaning and importance and helps define your priorities [passage #25] · [Identifying your values and a value action plan](topics/nd-mindfulness/identify-values-action-plan.md)
- **Vestibular** — the sense related to balance; an over-sensitive vestibular system can explain motion sickness [passage #71, #78] · [Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile](topics/nd-sensory-needs/sensory-overload-and-profile.md); [The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity](topics/nd-sensory-needs/hyper-and-hyposensitivity.md)
- **visual stimming** — tracing patterns in the environment, or a word or sentence in the air, with your finger or eyes as a mindfulness exercise [passage #33] · [Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation](topics/nd-mindfulness/accessible-mindfulness-exercises.md)
- **Warmline** — a phone line listed as an option to call when lonely or sad [passage #67] · [Check the Facts and Act Intentionally (neurodivergent-friendly Opposite Action)](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/check-facts-act-intentionally.md)
- **Window of Tolerance** — the optimal zone, coined by Dr. Dan Siegel, in which we are neither overstimulated/overwhelmed nor understimulated/underwhelmed and can stay regulated while feeling emotions [passage #62] · [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
- **Wise mind** — DBT state of mind combining logical and emotional mind, respecting and listening to both; unique to each person [passage #21, #22] · [Wise Mind: balancing logical mind and emotional mind](topics/nd-mindfulness/wise-mind.md)
- **zone of tolerance** — the book's term for the middle, regulated state inside the window, unique to each person [passage #62, #63] · [The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people](topics/nd-emotional-regulation/window-of-tolerance-dysregulation.md)
