# NotebookLM pack — The Neurodivergent Friendly Workbook of DBT Skills

Self-contained study documents generated mechanically from the knowledge files (same full content, lighter markup, compact citations). Upload the file(s) for the domain you want to study, or all of them for a whole-channel notebook.

- `nd-dbt-everyday-wellbeing.md` — **Neurodivergent-friendly DBT and everyday well-being** (~3,522 words): DBT adapted for neurodivergent people: how the workbook works and choosing a skill by emotional intensity; Self-care, a guide to my needs and the support and accommodations wheel; Affirmations for neurodivergent people and for rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD)
- `nd-mindfulness.md` — **Mindfulness for neurodivergent minds** (~5,700 words): Mindfulness adapted: attention, interoception and aphantasia differences, when to use it, and reflection; Wise Mind: balancing logical mind and emotional mind; Identifying your values and a value action plan; Self check-in: noticing emotions, body, energy, breathing and focus, then acting on it; Accessible mindfulness exercises: senses, music, stimming, anchor objects, pets and mindful appreciation
- `nd-distress-tolerance.md` — **Distress tolerance and meltdowns** (~5,244 words): Distress tolerance basics: the STOP skill and a personal crisis plan; Calming the body fast: mammalian diving reflex and the TIPP skill; Managing meltdowns: signs, aftercare, prevention, Meltdown Mapping and a tip sheet for loved ones; Sensory self-soothing, IMPROVE the environment, stims and special interests as regulation
- `nd-emotional-regulation.md` — **Emotional regulation** (~5,022 words): Understanding emotions: their role, the emotional triangle and emotional responses; Alexithymia and describing your emotions; The Window of Tolerance and emotional dysregulation in neurodivergent people; Check the Facts and Act Intentionally (neurodivergent-friendly Opposite Action)
- `nd-sensory-needs.md` — **Managing sensory needs** (~3,631 words): The eight senses: recognising and managing hypersensitivity and hyposensitivity; Sensory overload and mapping your sensory profile; Building a sensory toolkit and a sensory-friendly safe space

The library root also holds ready-made cross-channel sets: `index/notebooklm/<area>/` gathers the NotebookLM files of every channel on one subject (open the hub or a channel library and press **NotebookLM** on a topic).
