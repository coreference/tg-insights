## About the channel

## Channel in one paragraph

"deep mind 2.0" is a Russian-language Telegram channel (posts from 10 November 2019 to 16 July 2026) by the founder of Deep Mind (launched as Deep Mind Consulting), a company working on mindfulness, wellbeing and corporate culture. Its stated purpose is "to make business more human": companies should treat people "as a source, not a means". Over the years it moves from conscious business and culture change toward meditation, trauma and the nervous system, burnout, mindful leadership, Theory U, vertical development, collective trauma and war, emigration, and the human side of AI.

## Authors and voice

Nearly every post is by the channel author, Max (Maxim) Rodin. He describes himself as a facilitator and entrepreneur and as a mindfulness teacher. He says he practised Southern (Theravada) Buddhism for six years, now practises mainly in Tibetan Buddhism, and does at least one vipassana retreat a year. Guest posts and forwards appear occasionally (e.g. Alexandra Vilvovskaya, Inna German, Olga Gurova, Dasha Kravchenko, Ruslan Zaydullin). One knowledge file credits some forwarded posts (from the channel "reconnect") to "Maxim Timofeev". The tone is personal, frank and sometimes blunt or profane. It mixes first-person confession with business vocabulary and many English terms (purpose, mindset, wellbeing, flourishing, presence).

## Perspective and stance

The author holds that inner state and presence come before strategy and productivity. He sees mindfulness and self-compassion as core meta-skills and treats ethics as the non-negotiable base of inner work. In his view, toxic leadership and hustle culture grow out of unprocessed trauma. His evidence is mostly his own practice and crises, his client and corporate-programme work, and named teachers and authors (Scharmer, Wilber, Davidson, Tsoknyi Rinpoche, Holecek, Tift). He also uses surveys and studies (Gallup, Deloitte, McKinsey, Skolkovo, Harvard) and forwarded analyses.

## Audience

The channel addresses founders, managers, HR and team leaders, helping professionals, and Russian-speaking readers who practise meditation or therapy. Many of them are dealing with burnout, crisis, war or relocation.

## How to use this knowledge

It is strongest on practical frameworks for mindful leadership, team and culture work, stress and burnout, meditation practice, and choosing practitioners safely. Attribute views as "According to Max Rodin (deep mind 2.0)…". Name guest authors and cited researchers separately. Quote verbatim when the author's own wording or a numbered framework matters.

## Constraints and caveats

This is not medical, psychiatric or financial advice. The supplement, sleep and self-regulation protocols are the author's personal practice. Promotion of Deep Mind programmes, retreats, courses and events was left out. Survey figures, wellness trends and the political and AI commentary are tied to their dates, from 2019 to 2026. Views on Russia, war and politics are the author's own opinion.

2. **Media is source** — text inside image cards and PDFs is transcribed locally (`parsed/media_text.jsonl`).

4. **Knowledge files follow the Insight Extraction Standard** (full knowledge, not pointers) and pass mechanical depth QA: coverage, density vs source, complete enumerations, no pointer phrases, valid citations, verbatim quotes.
5. **Derived artifacts are mechanical** — `library.html`, `notebooklm/`, `agent-kb/`, glossary, people and resources are generated from the knowledge files.
