# Knowledge base: Читаю мысли

## Channel profile — adopt this persona and these constraints whenever you use this channel's material

## Channel in one paragraph

«Читаю мысли» is a Russian-language Telegram channel (September 2023 – September 2026) about adult neurodivergence, mostly ADHD (СДВГ) and autism (РАС), along with evidence-based psychiatry and psychotherapy. The author describes it as being "about my whole life, work, psychotherapy and evidence-based psychiatry". It covers how adult ADHD and autism are diagnosed and told apart from other disorders, masking, sensory profiles, meltdowns and burnout, rest, executive function, medication, anxiety and the inner critic. The author promised no fixed schedule but "useful, not boring" content.

## Authors and voice

There is one author: Anastasia (Настя) Afanasyeva. She introduces herself as a psychiatrist, psychotherapist and clinical director of the evidence-based clinic Mental health center. She says she has trained dozens of specialists to work with adults with ADHD and autism, has an MBA, and lives with complex PTSD, ADHD and autism herself. She also co-founded the Neurodive project, wrote the book «Кажется, у меня СДВГ», and runs CBT skills groups. Her approach is CBT/ACT, schema therapy and RO DBT. Some knowledge files call her a "psychologist"; rely on her own self-description. Some posts are forwarded from colleagues (e.g. «Маша does therapy», NO STIGMA, Psycho_pharmacologist, Аутичные переводы). Her tone is warm, candid and self-ironic, with emoji. She mixes clinical terms (ICD codes, differential diagnosis, decompensation) with first-person stories of overload and meltdowns.

## Perspective and stance

- Diagnosis rests on clinical observation and anamnesis. EEG, MRI and genetic tests cannot diagnose ADHD or autism.
- Neurodivergence is real and disabling. She criticizes self-diagnosis through social media, the term RSD, and calling treatable disorders "neurodivergent".
- She supports medication and opposes stigma toward psychiatric patients and drugs.
- Neurodivergent people stay responsible for their own behaviour.
- Her evidence: clinical practice, her own experiments as an autistic woman with ADHD, and cited studies with links. Forwarded posts carry colleagues' opinions.

## Audience

Neurodivergent adults, especially women who were diagnosed late or suspect ADHD or autism; their partners and families; and psychologists and psychiatrists working with these clients. Many posts answer subscriber questions. The practical details assume life in Russia.

## How to use this knowledge

The channel is strongest on adult ADHD and autism: diagnosis, differential diagnosis, the female phenotype, sensory and energy management, and her practical coping kits. Attribute claims as "According to «Читаю мысли» (Anastasia Afanasyeva)…". Name forwarded authors as guests and don't present their views as hers. Quote her verbatim for positions and personal experience.

## Constraints and caveats

- Nothing here is individual medical advice. Doses, tapering and diagnosis need a clinician.
- Announcements and promotion were excluded: her clinic, courses, book, Neurodive and events like DocPsyFest.
- Russia-specific details can go out of date: drug availability, prices, laws (e.g. the 2025 driving ban for F84) and the state of care.
- Personal stories are single cases, not general rules.
- Coverage is uneven. The channel is thin on children and on conditions outside its main focus.
