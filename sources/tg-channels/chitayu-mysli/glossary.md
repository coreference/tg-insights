Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **"Зонтик" нейроотличий** — the "neurodivergent umbrella": activist notion of which conditions count as neurodivergence [msg #75] · [Differential diagnosis: ADHD and autism vs bipolar, depression, BPD and OCPD](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/neurodivergence-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **"сначала человек"** — person-first terminology ("person with autism"), preferred by many parents [msg #717] · [Autism spectrum: support levels, genetics and history](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-support-levels-genetics.md)
- **1 степень поддержки** — level 1 support need (autism) [msg #277, #281, #288, #603] · [How adult ADHD and autism are diagnosed](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/adult-adhd-autism-diagnosis.md); [Masking, compensation and unmasking](topics/masking-social-life/masking-unmasking.md); [Meltdowns, shutdowns and acute overload](topics/overload-meltdowns-burnout/meltdowns-shutdowns-overload.md)
- **AAC (augmentative and alternative communication)** — tools such as picture symbols used for communication, typical support at level 3 [msg #151, #153] · [Autism spectrum: support levels, genetics and history](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-support-levels-genetics.md)
- **BDNF** — brain-derived neurotrophic factor, named on the author's slide as the link between movement and neuron growth [msg #787] · [Behavioral activation, movement and mindfulness for mood](topics/anxiety-mood-coping/mood-activation-mindfulness.md)
- **Default Mode Network (DMN)** — network of the brain's passive mode, central to the sense of "I" and continuity of self [msg #733] · [Executive dysfunction, environment and work](topics/adhd-self-management/executive-dysfunction-work.md)
- **DIVA-5** — Diagnostic Interview for ADHD in adults, used in the Dutch study [msg #618] · [ADHD and autism in women: phenotype, masking and hormones](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/women-adhd-autism.md)
- **DMDD** — disruptive mood dysregulation disorder (childhood) [msg #749] · [Differential diagnosis: ADHD and autism vs bipolar, depression, BPD and OCPD](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/neurodivergence-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **Executive Network** — ventral prefrontal network for self-reflection and conscious control [msg #733] · [Executive dysfunction, environment and work](topics/adhd-self-management/executive-dysfunction-work.md)
- **F 90.0 Нарушение активности и внимания** — ICD-10 code "Disturbance of activity and attention", the official code for ADHD, located in the childhood-onset rubric [msg #124] · [How adult ADHD and autism are diagnosed](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/adult-adhd-autism-diagnosis.md)
- **F84 (МКБ-10) «Общие расстройства психологического развития»** — ICD-10 section of pervasive developmental disorders incl. autism and Asperger syndrome, now in the list of conditions barring driving [msg #728] · [Self-diagnosis, myths and stigma around neurodivergence](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/neurodivergence-myths-stigma.md)
- **FLORIDA Y-BOCS** — Florida Yale-Brown Obsessive Compulsive Scale, a scale patients fill in at home to structure observations of OCD symptoms [msg #772] · [Anxiety, phobias, panic and OCD: exposure and self-observation](topics/anxiety-mood-coping/anxiety-exposure-ocd.md)
- **Neurodive** — course for specialists run by Afanasyeva and Maria Novikova [msg #603] · [How adult ADHD and autism are diagnosed](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/adult-adhd-autism-diagnosis.md)
- **RFT (теория реляционных фреймов)** — relational frame theory - a framework model of how people establish and understand relations between words and concepts in language [msg #625] · [Inner critic, schema therapy and soothing the threat system](topics/inner-critic-self-relationship/inner-critic-schema-therapy.md)
- **Salience Network** — network deciding which stimuli matter and switching attention between inner and outer tasks [msg #733] · [Executive dysfunction, environment and work](topics/adhd-self-management/executive-dysfunction-work.md)
- **«глюк переключения»** — "switching glitch": in ADHD, a hang between a finished task and a not-yet-loaded new one [msg #689] · [Meltdowns, shutdowns and acute overload](topics/overload-meltdowns-burnout/meltdowns-shutdowns-overload.md)
- **«горячий» контур** — the "hot" circuit — name for the "Why" circuit because it is tied to emotions [msg #66] · [Executive dysfunction, environment and work](topics/adhd-self-management/executive-dysfunction-work.md)
- **агрегаторы** — aggregators — specialist-listing platforms; the author finds them problematic [msg #600] · [Choosing specialists and evidence-based mental health care](topics/psychiatric-treatment-care/choosing-mental-health-care.md)
- **адвокат психического здоровья** — mental-health advocate [msg #38] · [Self-diagnosis, myths and stigma around neurodivergence](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/neurodivergence-myths-stigma.md)

- **аллистичные люди** — allistic people – anyone not autistic, i.e. neurotypical or non-autistic neurodivergent [msg #67] · [Autism spectrum: support levels, genetics and history](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-support-levels-genetics.md)
