# Glossary — This is too much

Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **"быстрые" углеводы** — "fast" carbohydrates — e.g. white bread, pelmeni; linked to blood-sugar spikes and mood swings [msg #32] · [Lowering emotional vulnerability: routines, food and boring basics](topics/dbt-skills/emotional-vulnerability-self-care-basics.md)
- **"волшебная пыльца"** — "magic dust" (as in Peter Pan); the author's metaphor for self-awareness, which turns scattered facts about oneself into a whole self-image [msg #87] · [Self-image, identity and emptiness in BPD](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-self-image-emptiness.md)
- **"Я" / чувство "Я"** — the self, the sense of "I"; one's identity, from which self-esteem grows [msg #87] · [Self-image, identity and emptiness in BPD](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-self-image-emptiness.md)
- **Attention Restoration Theory (ART)** — theory that directed attention is effortful and tiring, and some environments let it rest [msg #247] · [Procrastination, motivation and the vacation brain](topics/motivation-procrastination/procrastination-motivation-vacation.md)
- **Borderpolar** — informal name for the co-occurrence of BPD and bipolar disorder in one person [msg #131, #133] · [Differential diagnosis and classification: BPD, bipolar, cPTSD and HiTOP](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/differential-diagnosis-classification.md)
- **CBT-I** — cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia [msg #244] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **controlling interpersonal strategies (controlling-caregiving, controlling-punitive)** — ways of influencing another's behaviour to obtain or keep the needed closeness and availability [msg #267] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **EES (emotional empathy scale)** — scale the author used in her empathy-testing bot; original study mean 33 [msg #228] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **emotional amplification / эмоциональное усиление** — intensifying emotions (positive or negative) by thinking about them repeatedly [msg #238] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **Enduring Somatic Threat Model (EST)** — model of medical trauma in which the threat is internal and continues over time [msg #253] · [Recognizing PTSD and complex PTSD](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/ptsd-cptsd-recognition.md)
- **favorite person (FP)** — the person someone with BPD relies on heavily for emotional support, attention and validation, idealises and looks up to; the centre of their attention and attachment, more than a best friend [msg #113] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **frustrative non-reward** — frustration caused by not receiving an expected reward or result [msg #260] · [Understanding emotions and emotional dysregulation](topics/emotions-dysregulation/understanding-emotional-dysregulation.md)
- **GAD-7** — a 7-question screening questionnaire for GAD [msg #114] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **gene–environment-developmental model** — explanation of behaviour as individual vulnerability meeting a specific environment and developmental experience [msg #267] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **habituation** — older model of exposure: fear decreases through getting used to the situation [msg #250] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **HiTOP (Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology)** — empirically derived, dimensional, hierarchical classification of mental disorders [msg #211, #212] · [Differential diagnosis and classification: BPD, bipolar, cPTSD and HiTOP](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/differential-diagnosis-classification.md)
- **Imagery Rehearsal Therapy (IRT) / терапия репетиции образов** — rewriting a nightmare by day and rehearsing the safe version [msg #244] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **inhibitory learning** — current model of exposure: new learning that does not erase the "this is dangerous" association but forms a competing one [msg #250] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **intolerance of aloneness / непереносимость одиночества** — difficulty tolerating being alone; linked to how the social circle of a person with BPD is structured [msg #251] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **IPSRT (Interpersonal and Social Rhythm Therapy)** — evidence-based therapy developed specifically for bipolar disorder that stabilizes circadian rhythms and daily routines and works on interpersonal stress [msg #154, #220] · [Bipolar disorder: routines, caffeine and pregnancy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/bipolar-disorder-management.md)
- **jeong (정)** — Korean word for attachment to a close friend; example of a precise emotion concept [msg #187] · [Understanding emotions and emotional dysregulation](topics/emotions-dysregulation/understanding-emotional-dysregulation.md)
- **Lucid Dream Therapy (LDT)** — therapy based on controlling lucid dreams [msg #244] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **luminiscing** — "positive rumination": getting stuck in thoughts that feel illuminating and revelatory, mostly pleasant (explicitly not a scientific term) [msg #237] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **maladaptive daydreaming** — intense, compulsive positive fantasising for hours that functions as escape from reality and harms it [msg #238] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **MBT (Mentalization-Based Treatment)** — therapy for BPD built on mentalization; effective, but according to the author it gets weaker results than DBT [msg #198] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **N1, N2, N3** — NREM stages: very light transitional sleep, more stable sleep, deep slow-wave sleep [msg #256] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **PCL-R** — the special scale used to assess psychopathy as a research construct [msg #229] · [Antisocial personality disorder: signs, myths and therapy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/antisocial-personality-disorder.md)
- **Rejection sensitive dysphoria / дисфория, чувствительная к отвержению** — intense, overwhelming emotional pain in response to rejection, criticism or refusal, felt physically [msg #66] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **REM-behavior disorder / расстройство поведения в REM-сне** — partially acting out dream content because REM muscle inhibition fails [msg #255] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **REM-сон / быстрый сон** — REM sleep: phase of the most vivid dreams, emotional and memory processing, muscle atonia [msg #147, #241] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **reverie** — gentle, self-absorbed "floating" in thoughts, images and fantasies on one's favourite topics [msg #238] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **reward-focused attention / cognitive elaboration** — the brain repeatedly focusing on a pleasurable stimulus and reactivating its emotions [msg #238] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **SAD (seasonal affective disorder) / сезонное депрессивное расстройство** — a variant (specifier) of depression with a seasonal pattern, not a separate diagnosis [msg #83] · [Depression, seasonal depression and grief](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/depression-seasonal-grief.md)
- **Self-Determination Theory (SDT), теория самодетерминации** — motivation theory that distinguishes several bases (reasons) for doing something [msg #266] · [Procrastination, motivation and the vacation brain](topics/motivation-procrastination/procrastination-motivation-vacation.md)
- **soft fascination, мягкое привлечение внимания** — stimuli that gently capture attention without demanding effortful focus (water, trees, clouds, unfamiliar streets) [msg #247] · [Procrastination, motivation and the vacation brain](topics/motivation-procrastination/procrastination-motivation-vacation.md)
- **somatic threat cues** — bodily sensations that, after a medical event, act as reminders of danger [msg #253] · [Recognizing PTSD and complex PTSD](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/ptsd-cptsd-recognition.md)
- **Unified Protocol** — transdiagnostic therapy protocol for affective disorders organized by problem type rather than diagnosis [msg #211] · [Differential diagnosis and classification: BPD, bipolar, cPTSD and HiTOP](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/differential-diagnosis-classification.md)
- **zeitgeber («зайтгибер»)** — from German "time-giver": a cue that tells the body what time it is, whether it is day or night — natural (sunrise, sunset) and social (work schedule, meals, neighbours, notifications) [msg #220] · [Bipolar disorder: routines, caffeine and pregnancy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/bipolar-disorder-management.md)
- **«туман в голове»** — "brain fog": scattered attention and slowed thinking in depression [msg #139, #140] · [Depression, seasonal depression and grief](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/depression-seasonal-grief.md)
- **автобиографическая память** — autobiographical memory; memory of past experience plus the links between memories and how one showed oneself in them; an indirect indicator of identity integration [msg #87] · [Self-image, identity and emptiness in BPD](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-self-image-emptiness.md)
- **адаптивное совладание** — adaptive coping: different perspectives in one's social circle help change the negative impact of intrusive trauma-related thoughts; linked to PTG [msg #78] · [Coping with trauma, ongoing threat and post-traumatic growth](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/trauma-coping-ongoing-threat.md)
- **активные мысли о самоубийстве** — active suicidal thoughts; thoughts that include concrete plans or intent to harm oneself, usually with clear warning signs [msg #106] · [Suicidal thinking and self-harm](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/suicidal-thinking-self-harm.md)
- **алекситимия** — alexithymia; difficulty recognizing one's own emotions [msg #178] · [Suicidal thinking and self-harm](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/suicidal-thinking-self-harm.md)
- **аллостатическая нагрузка** — allostatic load — cumulative physiological stress burden, a risk factor for chronic pain [msg #165] · [Recognizing PTSD and complex PTSD](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/ptsd-cptsd-recognition.md)
- **амигдала / миндалина** — amygdala — the brain's fear and threat centre [msg #147, #241] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **амотивация (amotivation)** — lack of sufficient intention to act (result seems unimportant, no belief in coping, no perceived link between effort and outcome) [msg #266] · [Procrastination, motivation and the vacation brain](topics/motivation-procrastination/procrastination-motivation-vacation.md)
- **антисоциальное расстройство личности (АРЛ)** — antisocial personality disorder (ASPD); an official diagnosis in DSM-5 and ICD-11 (МКБ-11) [msg #229] · [Antisocial personality disorder: signs, myths and therapy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/antisocial-personality-disorder.md)
- **архитектура сна** — sleep architecture — duration and alternation of sleep stages [msg #255] · [PTSD nightmares and sleep disturbances](topics/trauma-and-sleep/ptsd-nightmares-sleep.md)
- **атипичная депрессия** — atypical depression: mood reactivity to positive events plus oversleeping, heaviness, sensitivity to criticism [msg #144] · [Depression, seasonal depression and grief](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/depression-seasonal-grief.md)
- **аффективный (эмоциональный) реализм** — affective realism: taking one's feelings as facts about the world [msg #187] · [Understanding emotions and emotional dysregulation](topics/emotions-dysregulation/understanding-emotional-dysregulation.md)
- **БАР (биполярное аффективное расстройство)** — bipolar affective disorder, bipolar disorder [msg #25, #131, #151, #154, #220] · [Bipolar disorder: routines, caffeine and pregnancy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/bipolar-disorder-management.md); [Differential diagnosis and classification: BPD, bipolar, cPTSD and HiTOP](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/differential-diagnosis-classification.md)
- **безоценочность** — non-judgmentalness — noticing evaluative, black-and-white statements and rephrasing them non-judgmentally [msg #50] · [Mindfulness in DBT: WHAT skills, non-judgment and urge surfing](topics/dbt-skills/dbt-mindfulness-skills.md)
- **беспокойство** — worry: in this text not an emotion but an intrusive, repetitive thinking process that often goes with anxiety (what may go wrong, how to avoid it, what to choose, what if the wrong choice was made) [msg #114] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **бессилие** — powerlessness / helplessness — a complex feeling that is a reaction to another emotion that cannot be relieved [msg #104] · [Crisis survival skills: STOP, TIPP and distraction](topics/dbt-skills/dbt-crisis-survival-skills.md)
- **биосоциальная модель** — biosocial model of BPD development, stressing the role of the environment [msg #17, #86, #191] · [BPD: definition, causes and explanatory models](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-definition-causes-models.md); [Self-image, identity and emptiness in BPD](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-self-image-emptiness.md)
- **бюджет организма** — body budget: the brain's forecasting and allocation of the body's energy [msg #187] · [Understanding emotions and emotional dysregulation](topics/emotions-dysregulation/understanding-emotional-dysregulation.md)
- **валидация** — validation: acknowledging and accepting another's (or one's own) thoughts, feelings, sensations and behaviour as understandable and legitimate, without implying agreement [msg #75] · [Radical acceptance and validation](topics/dbt-skills/radical-acceptance-validation.md)
- **внешняя система поддержки (external support system)** — the author's term for the FP as an outside source of safety and calm that the person with BPD cannot provide internally [msg #113] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **внутренний критик** — inner critic; the inner voice producing self-critical thoughts, which can be given a name [msg #48] · [Self-criticism, people-pleasing and social comparison](topics/emotions-dysregulation/self-criticism-people-pleasing.md)
- **внутренняя мотивация** — intrinsic motivation — the activity is interesting or enjoyable in itself [msg #266] · [Procrastination, motivation and the vacation brain](topics/motivation-procrastination/procrastination-motivation-vacation.md)
- **возвращение души** — return of the soul; shamanic rite restoring connection with body and nature and wholeness [msg #230] · [How religions and spiritual traditions made sense of trauma](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/religion-spiritual-traditions-trauma.md)
- **волшебная таблетка** — "magic pill" — the wished-for instant, effortless fix (insight, secret technique) [msg #99] · [Lowering emotional vulnerability: routines, food and boring basics](topics/dbt-skills/emotional-vulnerability-self-care-basics.md)
- **восходящее социальное сравнение** — upward social comparison; comparing oneself with someone who has what one lacks [msg #262] · [Self-criticism, people-pleasing and social comparison](topics/emotions-dysregulation/self-criticism-people-pleasing.md)
- **высокофункциональное ПРЛ** — high-functioning BPD — popular idea of BPD people who cope with work, study and daily life without visible difficulty; per the channel not a separate type of disorder [msg #130] · [BPD: definition, causes and explanatory models](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-definition-causes-models.md)
- **вытеснение** — repression — pushing out of memory what does not fit; can replace dissociation after the event [msg #118, #124] · [Neurobiology of trauma and trauma memory](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/trauma-neurobiology-memory.md)
- **газлайтинг** — gaslighting — strategy of making another doubt their perception, judgments or memories; not a scientific term [msg #125] · [Neurobiology of trauma and trauma memory](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/trauma-neurobiology-memory.md)
- **генерализованное тревожное расстройство (ГТР)** — generalised anxiety disorder (GAD): a disorder in which anxiety is triggered by many different everyday things, with endless worrying thoughts [msg #114] · [Avoidance, rumination, anxiety and exposure](topics/emotions-dysregulation/avoidance-rumination-exposure.md)
- **гипердиагностика** — overdiagnosis, named as the context for asking whether a person truly has both cPTSD and BPD [msg #202] · [Differential diagnosis and classification: BPD, bipolar, cPTSD and HiTOP](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/differential-diagnosis-classification.md)
- **гипердопаминергическое состояние** — hyperdopaminergic state (a lot of dopamine), described as existing in BD [msg #151] · [Bipolar disorder: routines, caffeine and pregnancy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/bipolar-disorder-management.md)
- **гиперментализация (hypermentalization)** — a tendency to build excessive, inadequate models of what is happening inside another person; false certainty that we know what the other thinks and feels [msg #3] · [BPD in relationships: rejection sensitivity, mentalization and loneliness](topics/borderline-personality-disorder/bpd-interpersonal-hypersensitivity.md)
- **гипоталамо-гипофизарно-надпочечниковая ось** — hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal (HPA) axis, one of the stress systems activated by chronic load [msg #247] · [Procrastination, motivation and the vacation brain](topics/motivation-procrastination/procrastination-motivation-vacation.md)
- **гиппокамп** — hippocampus — structure needed to consolidate memory from short- to long-term [msg #120] · [Neurobiology of trauma and trauma memory](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/trauma-neurobiology-memory.md)
- **датчик дыма** — smoke detector: metaphor for emotions as an alarm that rings when the brain thinks something must urgently be fixed, but often misfires [msg #57] · [Understanding emotions and emotional dysregulation](topics/emotions-dysregulation/understanding-emotional-dysregulation.md)
- **ДБТ** — диалектико-поведенческая терапия, dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) [msg #36, #229] · [Antisocial personality disorder: signs, myths and therapy](topics/diagnosis-mood-disorders/antisocial-personality-disorder.md); [Mindfulness in DBT: WHAT skills, non-judgment and urge surfing](topics/dbt-skills/dbt-mindfulness-skills.md)
- **ДБТ (диалектико-поведенческая терапия)** — DBT, dialectical behaviour therapy — evidence-based therapy for borderline personality disorder, rich in emotion-regulation skills [msg #15, #27] · [Crisis survival skills: STOP, TIPP and distraction](topics/dbt-skills/dbt-crisis-survival-skills.md); [Understanding emotions and emotional dysregulation](topics/emotions-dysregulation/understanding-emotional-dysregulation.md)

- **дейдриминг** — daydreaming: getting stuck in daydreams, one of the indications for 5-4-3-2-1 [msg #146] · [Dissociation, depersonalization and out-of-body experiences](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/dissociation-depersonalization.md)

- **диагностический критерий “А”** — diagnostic criterion A — presence of traumatic episodes, the starting point of PTSD/cPTSD diagnosis [msg #96] · [Recognizing PTSD and complex PTSD](topics/trauma-ptsd-cptsd/ptsd-cptsd-recognition.md)
