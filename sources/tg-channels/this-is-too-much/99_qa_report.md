## Limits a human should know

- Depth QA is mechanical: it proves nothing substantive was *skipped* by volume and structure, not that every nuance was captured. Spot-read against `library.html` (citations expand to the original message).
- Text from images/PDFs comes from local OCR (`parsed/media_text.jsonl`); OCR can misread stylised fonts — image-derived content is cited as `[img #id]` / `[file #id]`.
- English glosses and paraphrase are the archivist's; only quoted original-language text is checked verbatim.
