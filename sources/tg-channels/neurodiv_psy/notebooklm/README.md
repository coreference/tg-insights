# NotebookLM pack — (нейро)разнообразная психология

Self-contained study documents generated mechanically from the knowledge files (same full content, lighter markup, compact citations). Upload the file(s) for the domain you want to study, or all of them for a whole-channel notebook.

- `understanding-neurodivergence.md` — **Understanding neurodivergence** (~9,431 words): The neurodiversity paradigm, terminology and myths about autism; AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict; Lesser-known autistic traits and co-occurring conditions
- `diagnosis-and-identity-1.md` — **Diagnosis and neurodivergent identity (part 1 of 2)** (~14,089 words): Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism
- `diagnosis-and-identity-2.md` — **Diagnosis and neurodivergent identity (part 2 of 2)** (~4,987 words): Self-diagnosis, impostor syndrome and accepting a neurodivergent identity
- `masking-overload-burnout.md` — **Masking, overload and burnout** (~6,853 words): Masking, the CAT-Q questionnaire and unmasking; Meltdowns, shutdowns and autistic social hangover; Neurodivergent burnout and chronic fatigue
- `communication-social-life.md` — **Communication and social life** (~3,970 words): Autistic and ADHD communication styles, AAC and helper tools
- `executive-function-daily-life.md` — **Executive function and daily life** (~11,687 words): Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies
- `work-study-relocation.md` — **Work, study and relocation** (~7,574 words): Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations; Autism and emigration
- `emotional-self-support.md` — **Emotional self-support** (~3,133 words): Self-compassion, comparison, shame and emotional literacy
- `getting-good-therapy.md` — **Getting good therapy** (~1,620 words): Making therapy work and spotting therapist red flags

The library root also holds ready-made cross-channel sets: `index/notebooklm/<area>/` gathers the NotebookLM files of every channel on one subject (open the hub or a channel library and press **NotebookLM** on a topic).
