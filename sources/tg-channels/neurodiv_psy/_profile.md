## Channel in one paragraph

«(нейро)разнообразная психология» (@ia_psy) is a Russian-language Telegram channel run by a neurodivergent psychologist-counsellor. It posts psychoeducational material for autistic people and people with ADHD, "(neuro)diverse memes", and advice on keeping mental health intact online. It covers the neurodiversity paradigm, AuDHD, adult screening and self-diagnosis, masking, meltdowns and burnout, communication, executive function, work and study, emigration, self-compassion and choosing a therapist. Posts run from 2023-03-15 to 2026-11-07.

## Authors and voice

All posts come from the author, Юлия (Юля) Ан. She describes herself as a psychologist with a research background in cognitive science (EEG, test development), who left a cognitive neuroscience master's at the University of Trento. She says she was diagnosed with ADHD (autumn 2021) and autism (2022) as an adult, so she lives with AuDHD. She counsels adults online using CBT and ACT. Much of the content is translated or forwarded: cards from English-language accounts (neurodivergent_insights, @autieselfcare, @allforthedopamine), posts from other Russian channels (Аспергейша, Чистые Когниции, Доказательная Жабля, Нейропанк), and long translations such as the ADHD Foundation guide, Samantha Craft's checklist and the CAT-Q, which she co-translated. Her tone is warm, accepting and informal, and often personal ("например, я"). Typical vocabulary: нейроотличный, нейротипичный, маскировка, выгорание, исполнительные функции.

## Perspective and stance

The channel holds that autism and ADHD cannot be separated from the person and are not illnesses to cure. In its view, much of the suffering comes from discrimination and environments that are not inclusive. It favours acceptance over awareness, rejects comparing people by "functioning level", and defends responsible self-diagnosis. Its evidence is a mix of cited studies (Sokolova 2017, Hull et al., the Swedish CATSS study), translated community material and the author's own lived experience.

## Audience

The channel is written for Russian-speaking neurodivergent adults, especially people who are self-identifying or were diagnosed late (women in particular), and for people looking for informed specialists.

## How to use this knowledge

Use it for Russian-language self-screening resources, plain explanations of neurodivergent experience, and practical strategies (accommodations, money, unmasking, burnout recovery). Write "According to «(нейро)разнообразная психология»…". When material was translated or forwarded, name the original author or channel. Quote verbatim when giving checklist items, questionnaire wording and definitions.

## Constraints and caveats

This is psychoeducation, not diagnosis or medical advice. Screening tools only point toward seeing a specialist. Promotion of the author's paid consultations and ADHD skills-training groups was left out. Note that the translated ADHD guide was funded by Takeda UK. Lists of specialists, links, prevalence figures and immigration laws are tied to when they were posted and may be out of date.
