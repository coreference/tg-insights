# Resources shared on the channel

Things a reader can use directly: attached files (PDF guides, checklists, audio), articles and studies, free guides, tools and apps, websites of projects and organisations, free videos and podcasts. ★ = recommended by the author. Each entry names its source message; the topic heading links to the knowledge file, whose section *Resources and links* lists the same items.

## Understanding neurodivergence → [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](../topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md)

- ★ **Locus Focus — AuDHD rest tool (locusfocus.space/audhd-rest)** (tool) — Free web tool: mark your current brain state and get short tips on what to do and why, aimed at people with combined autism and ADHD (AuDHD). The post gives it to readers as super convenient. [msg #361] → <https://locusfocus.space/audhd-rest/>

## Understanding neurodivergence → [Lesser-known autistic traits and co-occurring conditions](../topics/understanding-neurodivergence/autistic-traits-co-occurring-conditions.md)

- ★ **Express survey of 240 people with PDA (aspergers.ru)** (article) — Results of a quick survey of 240 people with pathological demand avoidance (PDA), listing the needs they report (control, predictability, quiet places, autonomy). Cited in the post as supporting data on what PDA looks like. [msg #196] → <https://aspergers.ru/node/561>
- ★ **Pure Cognitions (Чистые Когниции) — article on PMDD (premenstrual dysphoric disorder)** (article) — Article by Anna Bondarenko (edited by Olga Kravchenko) on the symptoms, causes and treatment of PMDD, a severe hormonal mood disorder, as a co-occurring condition in neurodivergent people. Shared by the channel as further reading. [msg #64] → <https://teletype.in/@purecognitions/qhvE8wH-wIt>
- ★ **IDRlabs asexuality test (Russian version)** (tool) — Online test for asexuality that the post offers to readers; the author says she does not know how good it is and asks readers to suggest a better one. [msg #387] → <https://www.idrlabs.com/ru/asexuality/test.php?>
- ★ **NeuroKnowHow — Adult dyspraxia questionnaire** (tool) — Free online test in English that helps assess dyspraxic traits from childhood to the present. Offered as a first step for those who suspect dyspraxia, with the caveat that it is not a diagnosis. [msg #319] → <https://neuroknowhow.com/dyspraxia/adult-dyspraxia-questionnaire/>
- ★ **PDA profile questionnaire (aspergers.ru)** (tool) — Self-assessment questionnaire for the PDA (pathological demand avoidance) profile, offered to readers who recognise themselves in the description. It is a self-check, not a diagnosis. [msg #196] → <https://www.aspergers.ru/node/469>
- ★ **The Neurocuriosity Club (Megan) — video on sensitivity to (in)justice** (video) — YouTube video in English in which the channel author Megan explains how she sees heightened sensitivity to justice and fairness as a neurodivergent trait. The channel admin found her view interesting and shares it as the basis of the post. [msg #315] → <https://youtu.be/2htPsRC1p3I?si=IhVDveKPCN4pvJwV>
