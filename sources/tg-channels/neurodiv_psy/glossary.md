# Glossary — (нейро)разнообразная психология

Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **"аспи"** — "Aspie": self-label derived from Asperger syndrome, which many countries no longer single out as a separate disorder [msg #366] · [Self-diagnosis, impostor syndrome and accepting a neurodivergent identity](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/self-diagnosis-identity-acceptance.md)
- **"модные диагнозы"** — "fashionable diagnoses": the worry that a diagnosis is a trend that "everyone has now" [msg #365] · [Self-diagnosis, impostor syndrome and accepting a neurodivergent identity](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/self-diagnosis-identity-acceptance.md)
- **90-й перцентиль** — 90th percentile of symptom scores, used as the threshold for high ADHD symptom level in the Swedish study [msg #337] · [Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/screening-diagnosis-adhd-autism.md)
- **alertness / «собранность»** — sustained level of attention and readiness, harder to maintain in ADHD [msg #341] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md)
- **ASRS** — Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale, 18-question self-screening in parts A and B [msg #209] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md)
- **CAT-Q (Опросник камуфляжа аутичных черт)** — Camouflaging Autistic Traits Questionnaire, a self-report measure of camouflaging for people over 16 [msg #189, #192] · [Masking, the CAT-Q questionnaire and unmasking](topics/masking-overload-burnout/masking-cat-q-unmasking.md)
- **low arousal environment** — sensory-friendly environment with a minimum of stimuli (quiet room, dimmed light, no background music/TV/radio, headphones allowed) [msg #193] · [Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations](topics/work-study-relocation/work-study-accommodations.md)
- **must have / should have / nice have** — layers of the workplace needs pyramid from critical to desirable [msg #371] · [Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations](topics/work-study-relocation/work-study-accommodations.md)
- **RAADS-R** — autism self-screening test the channel recommends starting with (hosted on aspergers.ru) [msg #10] · [Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/screening-diagnosis-adhd-autism.md)
- **small talk / смол ток** — light chatter about nothing, used only to avoid silence and awkwardness [msg #48, #51] · [Autistic and ADHD communication styles, AAC and helper tools](topics/communication-social-life/neurodivergent-communication-aac.md)
- **АКД / АДК / AAC (альтернативная и дополненная/дополнительная коммуникация)** — alternative and augmentative communication: non-verbal ways of communicating for people with speech difficulties; alternative replaces speech, augmentative adds to it [msg #48, #51, #90] · [Autistic and ADHD communication styles, AAC and helper tools](topics/communication-social-life/neurodivergent-communication-aac.md)
- **аккомодации** — accommodations: adjustments that help a person cope with triggering situations, e.g. noise-cancelling headphones [msg #12, #60, #109, #200, #328, #406, #413] · [Neurodivergent burnout and chronic fatigue](topics/masking-overload-burnout/neurodivergent-burnout-fatigue.md); [Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations](topics/work-study-relocation/work-study-accommodations.md); [Self-diagnosis, impostor syndrome and accepting a neurodivergent identity](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/self-diagnosis-identity-acceptance.md); [Stimming: functions, types, stim toys and responding to harmful stims](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/stimming-functions-stim-toys.md); [The neurodiversity paradigm, terminology and myths about autism](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/neurodiversity-paradigm-terminology.md)
- **аккомодации (специальные условия)** — accommodations / reasonable adjustments: special conditions a person can request, e.g. for an interview [msg #193] · [Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations](topics/work-study-relocation/work-study-accommodations.md)
- **активный участник** — active participant: how the card set says the client should see their role in therapy [msg #96] · [Making therapy work and spotting therapist red flags](topics/getting-good-therapy/therapy-tips-red-flags.md)
- **алекситимия** — alexithymia — difficulty distinguishing/naming one's own emotions [msg #299] · [Lesser-known autistic traits and co-occurring conditions](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/autistic-traits-co-occurring-conditions.md)
- **аллистики** — allistics; non-autistic people [msg #128] · [Meltdowns, shutdowns and autistic social hangover](topics/masking-overload-burnout/meltdowns-shutdowns-social-hangover.md)
- **анмаскинг** — unmasking: masking less in safe situations [msg #327] · [Self-diagnosis, impostor syndrome and accepting a neurodivergent identity](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/self-diagnosis-identity-acceptance.md)
- **анмаскинг / "снять маску"** — unmasking: behaving in the way most natural for oneself, where possible and safe [msg #111, #297] · [Neurodivergent burnout and chronic fatigue](topics/masking-overload-burnout/neurodivergent-burnout-fatigue.md)
- **анмаскинг / размаскировка** — unmasking: the process of hiding one's neurodivergent traits less in communication [msg #257] · [Masking, the CAT-Q questionnaire and unmasking](topics/masking-overload-burnout/masking-cat-q-unmasking.md)
- **асексуальность** — asexuality — absence of or reduced sexual attraction to other people; considered a sexual orientation [msg #386, #387] · [Lesser-known autistic traits and co-occurring conditions](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/autistic-traits-co-occurring-conditions.md)
- **асинхронное общение** — asynchronous communication: sending a message without expecting an immediate reply [msg #49, #51] · [Autistic and ADHD communication styles, AAC and helper tools](topics/communication-social-life/neurodivergent-communication-aac.md)
- **Ассимиляция** — Assimilation factor: strategies used to try to fit in with others in social situations [msg #192] · [Masking, the CAT-Q questionnaire and unmasking](topics/masking-overload-burnout/masking-cat-q-unmasking.md)
- **АуСДВГ** — AuDHD, combined autism and ADHD [msg #399] · [Autism and emigration](topics/work-study-relocation/autism-emigration.md)
- **АуСДВГ (AuDHD)** — term for autism and ADHD occurring together in one person [msg #65, #70] · [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md)
- **бинго-чарт** — bingo chart: checklist of ADHD/ASD-friendly job traits to tick off when job hunting [msg #306] · [Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations](topics/work-study-relocation/work-study-accommodations.md)
- **боди-даблинг / body doubling** — doing a task in the presence of another person (also online) to reduce procrastination [msg #302, #324] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md)
- **внутренний / интернализированный эйблизм** — internalized ableism: having absorbed the idea that being different is "bad" or "wrong" and applying it to oneself and other disabled/neurodivergent people [msg #224, #336] · [Self-diagnosis, impostor syndrome and accepting a neurodivergent identity](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/self-diagnosis-identity-acceptance.md)
- **внутренний критик** — inner critic; the unpleasant inner voice that insults and compares [msg #39, #43] · [Self-compassion, comparison, shame and emotional literacy](topics/emotional-self-support/self-compassion-shame-emotions.md)
- **временная слепота / слепота ко времени / временная агнозия** — time blindness / time agnosia: difficulty managing and sensing time, a symptom rather than a diagnosis [msg #324, #340] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md)
- **выгорание у нейроотличных** — neurodivergent burnout: a state of exhaustion that for neurodivergent people often arises as an inherent part of living in social contexts not adapted to them [msg #108] · [Neurodivergent burnout and chronic fatigue](topics/masking-overload-burnout/neurodivergent-burnout-fatigue.md)
- **гигиена сна** — sleep hygiene: self-help actions for better sleep [msg #209] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md); [Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/screening-diagnosis-adhd-autism.md)
- **гиперфиксация** — hyperfixation: intense, all-consuming interest lasting days to months (sometimes years), typically ADHD, driven by novelty and need for stimulation [msg #35, #37] · [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md)
- **гиперфокус** — hyperfocus: short (up to a few hours) state of deep concentration in which one cannot break away and neglects basic needs, typically ADHD [msg #36, #37] · [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md)
- **движение за нейроразнообразие** — neurodiversity movement: advocates for civil rights, equality, respect and full social inclusion of neurodivergent people [msg #12] · [The neurodiversity paradigm, terminology and myths about autism](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/neurodiversity-paradigm-terminology.md)
- **двойная стигма** — double stigma: being both autistic and an immigrant [msg #391] · [Autism and emigration](topics/work-study-relocation/autism-emigration.md)
- **диагностическая субституция** — diagnostic substitution: people who used to receive other diagnoses now receive an ASD diagnosis [msg #337] · [Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/screening-diagnosis-adhd-autism.md)
- **диагностическое интервью** — diagnostic interview, the main method of diagnosing ADHD, conducted by a specialist [msg #255] · [Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/screening-diagnosis-adhd-autism.md)
- **диспраксия** — dyspraxia — impaired ability of the brain to plan and coordinate physical movements, affecting gross and fine motor skills [msg #318, #319] · [Lesser-known autistic traits and co-occurring conditions](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/autistic-traits-co-occurring-conditions.md)
- **дневник успехов / дневник достижений** — success / achievement diary; a record of anything you are proud of, big or small [msg #39, #41] · [Self-compassion, comparison, shame and emotional literacy](topics/emotional-self-support/self-compassion-shame-emotions.md)
- **избегание требований** — demand avoidance: avoiding tasks because they are presented as demands [msg #374] · [Neurodivergent people at work and university: job fit and accommodations](topics/work-study-relocation/work-study-accommodations.md)
- **интероцепция** — interoception: ability to sense signals the body sends (hunger, thirst, need for the toilet, emotions) [msg #26] · [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md)
- **инфодампинг** — infodumping: talking at length and in detail, usually as a monologue, about a topic of interest, often without checking whether the listener is ready to listen [msg #25, #74, #126] · [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md); [Autistic and ADHD communication styles, AAC and helper tools](topics/communication-social-life/neurodivergent-communication-aac.md)
- **исполнительная дисфункция** — executive dysfunction - impairment of executive functions that leads to avoiding tasks regardless of whether they are liked [msg #58] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md)
- **исполнительные функции** — executive functions: a set of cognitive skills for planning, organising and goal-directed behaviour, and for controlling attention, impulses and emotions [msg #109, #110, #209] · [Executive dysfunction, ADHD skills training and daily-life strategies](topics/executive-function-daily-life/executive-function-adhd-strategies.md); [Neurodivergent burnout and chronic fatigue](topics/masking-overload-burnout/neurodivergent-burnout-fatigue.md); [Screening and diagnosis of adult ADHD and autism](topics/diagnosis-and-identity/screening-diagnosis-adhd-autism.md)
- **исполнительные функции / исполнительная дисфункция** — executive functions (organizing tasks, object permanence, memory, concentration, decision-making, attention regulation) and their impairment [msg #25, #26] · [AuDHD: how ADHD and autism overlap and conflict](topics/understanding-neurodivergence/audhd-adhd-autism-overlap.md)
