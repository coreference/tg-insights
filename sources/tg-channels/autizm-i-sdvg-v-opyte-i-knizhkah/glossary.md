# Glossary — Аутизм и СДВГ в опыте и книжках

Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **AAC** — augmentative and alternative communication device [msg #255] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **AQ** — Autism-spectrum Quotient, measure of autistic traits used as a baseline predictor in the Strasbourg RCT [msg #266, #293] · [DBT for autistic adults: rationale, adaptations and evidence](topics/therapy-coping-skills/dbt-for-autism.md); [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **ARFID** — avoidant/restrictive food intake disorder; screened by NIAS [msg #288] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **ASI (терапия сенсорной интеграции Айрес)** — Ayres Sensory Integration therapy: evidence-based practice for autistic children 4-12 [msg #253] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **ASMR** — Autonomous Sensory Meridian Response; videos whose sounds or whispers can cause tingling and relaxation [msg #75] · [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
- **AuDHD** — autism and ADHD together [msg #285] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **AuDHD (СДВГ+РАС)** — unofficial term for people who have both autism and ADHD [msg #82] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **baby steps** — breaking a big task into small stages [msg #114] · [Distress tolerance and self-care skills for neurodivergent people](topics/therapy-coping-skills/distress-tolerance-self-care.md)
- **body doubling** — practising together with another person instead of alone [msg #222, #263] · [Boredom, boring tasks and mindfulness for ADHD](topics/attention-executive-function/boredom-mindfulness-adhd.md); [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **CAT-Q (Опросник камуфляжа аутичных черт)** — Camouflaging Autistic Traits Questionnaire, a 25-item self-report measure of social camouflaging for people over 16 [msg #40, #42, #140] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **CATSS** — Child and Adolescent Twin Study in Sweden, data source for the Swedish symptoms-vs-diagnoses study [msg #245] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **CHD8, REST, ZFX** — regulatory "authoritarian" genes that control many other genes, highlighted in an autism gene-network study [msg #203] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **DERS (шкала определения трудностей в регуляции эмоций)** — Difficulties in Emotion Regulation Scale, 36-item questionnaire, a basic DBT outcome measure with six subscales; lower score = fewer regulation difficulties [msg #258, #266] · [DBT for autistic adults: rationale, adaptations and evidence](topics/therapy-coping-skills/dbt-for-autism.md)
- **detailed focus processing** — autistic nervous-system feature of strong focus on details, which then replay endlessly in PTSD [msg #55] · [Anxiety, PTSD and OCD in autistic people](topics/co-occurring-mental-health/anxiety-ptsd-ocd-autism.md)
- **Dunn's Model** — model of sensory processing by occupational therapist Winnie Dunn: threshold x behavioural response, four profiles [msg #225] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **EES** — Emotional Empathy Scale used in the channel's empathy bot [msg #240] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **FLORIDA Y-BOCS** — Florida Yale-Brown Obsessive Compulsive Scale, home-filled checklist used to structure OCD self-observation [msg #247] · [Anxiety, PTSD and OCD in autistic people](topics/co-occurring-mental-health/anxiety-ptsd-ocd-autism.md)
- **HiTOP (Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology)** — dimensional model that treats psychopathology as continuous traits that cluster into spectra, not yes/no categories [msg #243] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **HSD** — hypermobility spectrum disorder, hypermobility that impairs life without meeting hEDS criteria [msg #293] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **lore** — shared community mythology/knowledge of an autistic community [msg #64] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **low maintenance среда** — low-demand environment, a frequent PDA recommendation criticised as reinforcing helplessness about anxiety [msg #130] · [Pathological demand avoidance (PDA) and the PANDA approach](topics/overload-demands-burnout/pathological-demand-avoidance.md)
- **N2pc и Pd** — EEG components reflecting pulling attention towards an object (N2pc) and suppressing a distracting signal (Pd) [msg #280] · [Hyperfocus, special interests and monotropism](topics/attention-executive-function/hyperfocus-special-interests-monotropism.md)
- **Perceptual Template Model** — model of three attention mechanisms: stimulus enhancement, external noise exclusion, internal noise reduction [msg #224] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **Registered Report** — journal format where the paper is accepted before data collection with fixed hypotheses and design [msg #264] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **salience network** — brain network that directs attention to an object; more active in autistic people engaged in a favourite activity [msg #13] · [Hyperfocus, special interests and monotropism](topics/attention-executive-function/hyperfocus-special-interests-monotropism.md)
- **sensory issues / сенсорная чувствительность** — umbrella for autistic sensory differences; author prefers "sensory issues" because sensitivity can be raised or lowered [msg #3] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **TAS-20** — Toronto Alexithymia Scale, the alexithymia questionnaire used in the Oxford study [msg #281] · [Alexithymia and interoception in autism](topics/emotions-alexithymia/alexithymia-interoception.md)
- **theory of mind** — the ability to view a situation from another person's role; the author links difficulty lying to it [msg #8] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **VVS (система вестибулярного накопления скорости)** — vestibular velocity storage: brainstem-cerebellar network amplifying weak vestibular signals, less effective in adults with GI [msg #253] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **«я в домике»** — "I'm in my little house" (children's game phrase for being in a safe, untouchable zone); name of the safe-space rule [msg #19] · [Meltdowns and shutdowns: triggers, differences and recovery](topics/overload-demands-burnout/meltdowns-shutdowns.md)
- **агентность** — agency: the sense of being able to influence one's own life [msg #48] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **алекситимия** — alexithymia — difficulty recognizing and describing one's emotions and distinguishing them from bodily sensations; feelings exist but are hard to catch and name [msg #2, #32, #48, #159, #170, #176, #261, #266] · [Alexithymia and interoception in autism](topics/emotions-alexithymia/alexithymia-interoception.md); [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md); [DBT for autistic adults: rationale, adaptations and evidence](topics/therapy-coping-skills/dbt-for-autism.md); [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md); [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md); [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
- **амигдала / миндалевидное тело** — amygdala; brain structure involved in threat detection and emotion processing, the brain's "smoke detector" [msg #60] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **АРЛ** — antisocial personality disorder (ASPD) [msg #249] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **Ассимиляция** — Assimilation: CAT-Q factor — strategies for trying to fit in with others in social situations [msg #42] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **аутизм как культура и идентичность** — autism as culture and identity, distinct from autism as a diagnosis [msg #64] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **аутистическая инертность / аутистическая инерция** — autistic inertia: difficulty shifting attention so that starting, finishing or moving between tasks is very hard; described as more than executive dysfunction [msg #96, #280] · [Hyperfocus, special interests and monotropism](topics/attention-executive-function/hyperfocus-special-interests-monotropism.md)
- **аутистическая инерция** — autistic inertia; difficulty starting from rest, stopping while in motion, and switching between states [msg #263] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **аутичное выгорание** — autistic burnout: emotional, physical and psychological exhaustion from stressors, lasting weeks to years, with heightened sensory vulnerability and loss of skills/functioning [msg #23] · [Autistic and ADHD burnout, boom-or-bust and pacing](topics/overload-demands-burnout/autistic-burnout-pacing.md)
- **афантазия** — aphantasia — inability to create visual images in one's mind (in some people also sounds, smells and other senses) [msg #16, #20] · [Autism traits beyond the stereotypes: lesser-known features and aphantasia](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-lesser-known-traits.md)
- **БАД** — dietary supplement (биологически активная добавка) [msg #44] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **БАР** — bipolar affective disorder [msg #57] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **бей, беги, замри** — fight, flight, freeze — the threat reflex behind PDA escalation [msg #119] · [Pathological demand avoidance (PDA) and the PANDA approach](topics/overload-demands-burnout/pathological-demand-avoidance.md)
- **бинауральные ритмы** — binaural beats; two sound waves of different frequencies played simultaneously, one to each ear [msg #24] · [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
- **бухгалтерия энергии / energy accounting** — pacing method assigning energy costs and gains to activities against a daily energy balance [msg #169] · [Autistic and ADHD burnout, boom-or-bust and pacing](topics/overload-demands-burnout/autistic-burnout-pacing.md)
- **быстрая эскалация под давлением** — rapid escalation under pressure (label of the escalation pyramid) [msg #119] · [Pathological demand avoidance (PDA) and the PANDA approach](topics/overload-demands-burnout/pathological-demand-avoidance.md)
- **вербальное сообщество** — verbal community — the people whose naming practices we copy when learning words for objects and feelings [msg #176] · [Alexithymia and interoception in autism](topics/emotions-alexithymia/alexithymia-interoception.md)
- **вестибулярное чувство** — vestibular sense: sense of movement in space, balance [msg #3, #22, #31, #253] · [Autism traits beyond the stereotypes: lesser-known features and aphantasia](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-lesser-known-traits.md); [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **внимание** — attention — interoceptive dimension: how often and how strongly one attends to body signals (how often the radio is on and how loud) [msg #281] · [Alexithymia and interoception in autism](topics/emotions-alexithymia/alexithymia-interoception.md)
- **восприятие времени** — time perception; subjective experience of time passing and organising life by an internal clock [msg #86] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **временнАя слепота** — time blindness; inability to plan and keep future events in mind [msg #25] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **Вялый когнитивный темп (ВКТ), Sluggish Cognitive Tempo (SCT)** — a cluster of attention-related symptoms (daydreaming, slow processing, low energy); not yet a recognised separate disorder [msg #29] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **гиперконтроль** — overcontrol: a trait (not a diagnosis), common in autism, of suppressing emotions and needs so strongly that one loses contact with oneself [msg #242] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **гиперлексия** — hyperlexia, unexpectedly advanced reading skills in children far above age expectations [msg #32] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **гипермобильность суставов** — joint hypermobility, ligaments weaker so joints bend more than normal [msg #32, #293] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **гиперфокус** — hyperfocus: an umbrella term for total absorption in a task with intense concentration, when the world outside the task disappears; a consequence of difficulty shifting attention [msg #89, #96, #109, #280] · [Hyperfocus, special interests and monotropism](topics/attention-executive-function/hyperfocus-special-interests-monotropism.md)
- **гиперфокус / гиперфиксация** — hyperfocus / hyperfixation: absorbed flow state in which time, food, drink and sleep are forgotten [msg #168] · [Autistic and ADHD burnout, boom-or-bust and pacing](topics/overload-demands-burnout/autistic-burnout-pacing.md)
- **гиперчувствительность** — hypersensitivity: excessive reaction to sensory input, can lead to meltdown, overload, distress, pain [msg #3, #22] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **гипостимуляция** — hypostimulation, lack of stimulation; its border with boredom is questioned [msg #192] · [Boredom, boring tasks and mindfulness for ADHD](topics/attention-executive-function/boredom-mindfulness-adhd.md)
- **гипотеза алекситимии** — alexithymia hypothesis — Bird and Cook's 2013 idea that the so-called emotional deficits of autism are better explained by co-occurring alexithymia [msg #261] · [Alexithymia and interoception in autism](topics/emotions-alexithymia/alexithymia-interoception.md)
- **гипочувствительность** — hyposensitivity - sensory-processing difference requiring high sensory input to register stimulation [msg #3, #22, #194] · [Boredom, boring tasks and mindfulness for ADHD](topics/attention-executive-function/boredom-mindfulness-adhd.md); [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **гиппокамп** — hippocampus; marks emotionally important events in memory [msg #60] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **говорить "в людей", а не "с людьми"** — talking "at people" rather than "with people" — monologue rather than dialogue [msg #8] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **гравитационная неуверенность (ГН, GI)** — gravitational insecurity: fear of movement arising from vestibular dysfunction [msg #253] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md)
- **гСЭД / гЭДС (hEDS)** — hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, a hereditary connective-tissue disorder [msg #293] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **ГТР** — GAD, generalised anxiety disorder [msg #71] · [Anxiety, PTSD and OCD in autistic people](topics/co-occurring-mental-health/anxiety-ptsd-ocd-autism.md)
- **ДБТ (диалектико-поведенческая терапия)** — DBT, dialectical behaviour therapy; created as a therapy for chronic suicidality, self-harm and emotion dysregulation, most often used for borderline personality disorder [msg #2, #193, #228] · [DBT for autistic adults: rationale, adaptations and evidence](topics/therapy-coping-skills/dbt-for-autism.md)
- **двойная эмпатия** — double empathy, theory of mutual misunderstanding (Damian Milton) [msg #285] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **дейдриминг / мечтательность наяву** — daydreaming; absorption in one's thoughts that detaches from the outside world [msg #29] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **диагностическая субституция** — diagnostic substitution; people who used to receive another diagnosis now receive ASD [msg #245] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **дисавтономия** — dysautonomia, dysfunction of the autonomic nervous system [msg #172] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **дисфория чувствительная к отвержению** — rejection sensitive dysphoria: intense, sometimes physically felt emotional pain on facing disapproval or social rejection [msg #36] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **длинные некодирующие РНК (lncRNA)** — long non-coding RNAs; RNA molecules that do not code proteins but regulate other genes' activity [msg #203] · [Research on autism and ADHD: brain, genetics, diagnosis trends and reading](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-adhd-research-reading.md)
- **ДПТ (диалектико-поведенческая терапия)** — dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT), the therapy the ПЕРЕЖИТЬ acronym comes from [msg #66] · [Distress tolerance and self-care skills for neurodivergent people](topics/therapy-coping-skills/distress-tolerance-self-care.md)
- **думскроллинг** — doomscrolling, compulsive reading of bad news; lowers anxiety in the moment but maintains it overall [msg #68] · [Distress tolerance and self-care skills for neurodivergent people](topics/therapy-coping-skills/distress-tolerance-self-care.md)
- **Железная стена** — "iron wall", imagined barrier used to block unbearable feelings for the moment [msg #66] · [Distress tolerance and self-care skills for neurodivergent people](topics/therapy-coping-skills/distress-tolerance-self-care.md)
- **задержка фазы сна** — delayed sleep phase [msg #286] · [Physical comorbidities, sleep and hormones in autism and ADHD](topics/physical-health-body/physical-comorbidities-sleep-hormones.md)
- **идентичность / представление о своем "Я" / Селф** — identity / sense of self: a stable idea of who one is, which the author calls the base of self-esteem and inner stability [msg #48] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **иерархия преодоления дискомфорта** — discomfort hierarchy; list of uncomfortable situations ranked from least to most unpleasant, worked through from the bottom [msg #178, #180] · [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md); [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
- **Импульсивность** — impulsivity, DERS subscale: poor behaviour control under strong emotion [msg #258] · [DBT for autistic adults: rationale, adaptations and evidence](topics/therapy-coping-skills/dbt-for-autism.md)
- **интернализированные симптомы** — internalized symptoms - those not visible from outside (racing thoughts, inner restlessness, inability to relax) [msg #53] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **интернализованное / пассивное проявление** — internalised or passive presentation: quiet refusal, anxiety kept inside, difficulties masked [msg #118] · [Pathological demand avoidance (PDA) and the PANDA approach](topics/overload-demands-burnout/pathological-demand-avoidance.md)
- **интероцепция** — interoception — processing / sensing signals from inside the body (heartbeat, hunger, thirst, tension, need to use the toilet, pain, breathing) [msg #3, #22, #31, #46, #51, #168, #173, #261] · [Alexithymia and interoception in autism](topics/emotions-alexithymia/alexithymia-interoception.md); [Autism traits beyond the stereotypes: lesser-known features and aphantasia](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-lesser-known-traits.md); [Autistic and ADHD burnout, boom-or-bust and pacing](topics/overload-demands-burnout/autistic-burnout-pacing.md); [Sensory processing differences in autism: senses, profiles and theories](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-processing-differences.md); [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
- **инфодампинг** — infodumping — sharing a large volume of information on a topic of interest [msg #8, #64] · [Autistic communication, empathy and friendship](topics/masking-communication-social/autistic-communication-friendship.md)
- **информационный демпинг** — info-dumping; enthusiastic talking about a special interest [msg #57] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **исполнительная дисфункция** — executive dysfunction: impairment of the mental processes responsible for planning, organisation, memory and starting and finishing tasks [msg #96] · [Hyperfocus, special interests and monotropism](topics/attention-executive-function/hyperfocus-special-interests-monotropism.md)
- **исполнительная функция** — executive function; the set of skills for managing one's life, "being one's own manager" [msg #25] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **исполнительные функции** — executive functions; the "conductor" of the orchestra in the #289 metaphor [msg #289] · [AuDHD, differential diagnosis and self-report questionnaires](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/audhd-differential-diagnosis.md)
- **кататония** — catatonia — a state in which a person does not, or almost does not, respond to the outside world [msg #30] · [Autism traits beyond the stereotypes: lesser-known features and aphantasia](topics/understanding-diagnosing-neurodivergence/autism-lesser-known-traits.md)
- **КБТ (когнитивно-поведенческая терапия)** — CBT, cognitive behavioural therapy; in the author's framing assumes every emotion results from thoughts about an event [msg #2] · [DBT for autistic adults: rationale, adaptations and evidence](topics/therapy-coping-skills/dbt-for-autism.md)
- **книга правил** — the "rule book" a masking person keeps updating after each perceived misstep [msg #62] · [Anxiety, PTSD and OCD in autistic people](topics/co-occurring-mental-health/anxiety-ptsd-ocd-autism.md)
- **когнитивная реабилитация** — cognitive rehabilitation; a program training attention, motivation, organisation, impulsivity, anger management [msg #284] · [Executive function, autistic inertia, time perception and object permanence](topics/attention-executive-function/executive-function-inertia-time.md)
- **когнитивный рефрейминг** — cognitive reframing; challenging and reformulating a negative thought into a more realistic one [msg #178] · [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
- **Компенсация** — Compensation: CAT-Q factor — strategies that actively compensate for social difficulties [msg #42] · [Masking and camouflaging: forms, costs and measurement](topics/masking-communication-social/masking-camouflaging.md)
- **комплексное ПТСР** — complex PTSD: when the traumatic event lasted long or repeated, there may be more symptoms [msg #55] · [Anxiety, PTSD and OCD in autistic people](topics/co-occurring-mental-health/anxiety-ptsd-ocd-autism.md)
- **коричневый шум** — brown noise; low-frequency noise resembling thunder or a waterfall [msg #24, #75] · [Sensory regulation, stimming and sensory safety plans](topics/sensory-processing-regulation/sensory-regulation-safety-plans.md)
