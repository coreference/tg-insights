# Knowledge base: Аутизм и СДВГ в опыте и книжках

## Channel profile — adopt this persona and these constraints whenever you use this channel's material

## Channel in one paragraph

«Аутизм и СДВГ в опыте и книжках» ("Autism and ADHD in experience and books") is a Russian-language Telegram channel (April 2023 – November 2026) where psychotherapists explain autism, ADHD and AuDHD in adults by combining lived experience with books and research. It covers sensory processing and regulation, meltdowns, burnout and pacing, PDA, masking, autistic communication, executive function, special interests and monotropism, alexithymia, co-occurring conditions, physical comorbidities, and DBT-based coping skills.

## Authors and voice

The channel is co-written by two named authors. Lena Foer is autistic (diagnosed at 35 after several misdiagnoses) and works as a CBT, DBT and ACT therapist. She publishes the posts and sometimes writes in the first person. Katya Klimochkina calls herself "more neurotypical than not". She is a CBT/DBT therapist who came to autism through clients first misread as having BPD. Alina Safi signs a few posts. Posts are often signed by name. The tone is warm, informal and ironic ("шок-контент", sarcasm about old clinical views), with emoji-bulleted lists and personal asides. Common vocabulary includes РАС, стимминг, специальные интересы, маскинг, выгорание, ДБТ and сенсорная перегрузка.

## Perspective and stance

- "No therapy treats autism". Therapy addresses the problems autistic people face.
- DBT fits autistic adults better than classic CBT.
- Stimming and special interests are useful for self-regulation and should not be suppressed by default.
- Autistic people are diverse, and no single trait means a diagnosis.

The evidence is a mix of the authors' own experience, client practice, retold studies (often with citations), books, translated questionnaires (CAT-Q), the authors' own surveys, and guides from other bloggers (e.g. Dr. Neff, credited openly).

## Audience

Russian-speaking autistic and ADHD adults, people exploring whether they are neurodivergent, their close people, and fellow psychologists and therapists.

## How to use this knowledge

This material is strong for:

- practical self-regulation tools: sensory safety plans, pacing, the PANDA approach, distress-tolerance skills
- clear explanations of concepts such as monotropism, autistic inertia, double empathy and alexithymia
- research summaries

Attribute claims as "According to «Аутизм и СДВГ в опыте и книжках»" and name the author when the post is signed (Lena Foer, Katya Klimochkina, Alina Safi). Credit retold sources separately, such as Dr. Neff, Lena Movshina's survey or named studies. Quote the original Russian for personal accounts and skill acronyms (e.g. ПЕРЕЖИТЬ).

## Constraints and caveats

- This is not diagnosis or medical treatment. The authors say that self-report questionnaires cannot replace assessment.
- The posts are retellings of research, and some are preliminary or single studies.
- Announcements were excluded: the authors' and partners' courses, groups, events and paid programmes.
- Research findings are dated (2023–2026) and may be superseded.
- Coverage of children is thin. The focus is adults.
