## Channel in one paragraph

«Human Gazing Upon Machine» is a Russian-language Telegram channel (March 2022 – November 2026) about how people and their brains work: autism and neurodiversity, brain research, perception and the self, social psychology, evidence-based therapy and relationships, happiness and rest, AI and human–machine interfaces, and science careers. Early posts promise practical care for readers in hard times (#takecare: "important knowledge and practices that help regain strength") next to science and ethics discussions.

## Authors and voice

One author, Rita (Margarita), writes almost all posts in the first person. As the channel states it, she is a psychologist who has worked in clinical practice, was hired on the international online therapy platform Fitcy, lives in Belgrade, and is moving into neuroscience research (learning Python and MATLAB). Guest and co-authored formats include the autism-myths series (with Maria), the sensory relaxation and recovery series (with practitioner Olga Gorbacheva), and Maria Shut's guest series "Professional spectrum". The voice is explanatory and personal, with frequent irony and jokes. Long topics come as multi-part article series.

## Perspective and stance

Autism is treated as a difference rather than a deficit, using the double empathy problem, critiques of myths and "functioning" labels, and neuroinclusion at work. The author prefers evidence-based approaches (ACT, IBCT, RO DBT, DocPsyFest material) and debunks popular claims such as love languages, the left/right brain and unreplicated results. She opposes helplessness and authoritarian compliance. Her evidence comes from summarised studies and reviews with numbers, her own interpretations and extensions, lecture recaps, and personal experience (career moves, hobbies, life abroad).

## Audience

Russian-speaking readers interested in psychology and neuroscience: autistic and neurodivergent people and those around them, psychologists and clinicians, people considering science careers, and readers living through crisis or emigration.

## How to use this knowledge

The channel is strongest on autism (myths, double empathy, employment, sensory regulation), on research digests about the brain, and on frameworks from social psychology and therapy. Attribute claims as "According to «Human Gazing Upon Machine»…". Name guest or co-authors and cited researchers separately from the author's own reading. Quote verbatim when giving her interpretations or extensions, which are her views and not the studies' findings.

## Constraints and caveats

This is not medical, psychiatric or legal advice, and it does not replace therapy. Research news reflects the date of posting, and studies on mice or other animals are reported as such. Career details (Fitcy rates and terms, job sites) come from one person's experience in 2022–2024 and may be out of date. Announcements, festival and event promotion, personal life posts and logistics were excluded. Some articles (Telegraph links, videos) were not available, so a few frameworks are incomplete.
