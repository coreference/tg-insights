# Glossary — На балансе с Катей Стрельниковой

Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **3 «с»** — three S's: слова (words), спорт (sport), слёзы (tears) — Malika's stress management [msg #218] · [«На балансе с…» interview series](topics/community/interviews.md)
- **3F, 3H, 3S** — sub-division of the 8 personal hours: Family/Friends/Faith; Health/Hygiene/Hobby; Soul/Service/Smile [msg #124] · [Work-life balance in practice](topics/balance/work-life.md)
- **4 вида энергии** — four kinds of energy: physical, emotional, intellectual, spiritual [msg #127] · [Burnout: prevention and exit](topics/rest/burnout.md)
- **4 слоя отдыха** — four layers of rest — a model discussed in the podcast with Radmila [msg #505] · [Channel life and announcements](topics/community/channel-life.md)
- **42% на отдых** — the Nagoskis' arithmetic that about 42% of time (≈10 hours a day) is physiologically needed for rest [msg #579] · [Series: I'm 40 and worn out](topics/rest/tired-series.md)
- **Action / Действие** — stage 4, "just do it" phase, relapses expected [msg #489] · [Why change is hard](topics/change/why-change-is-hard.md)
- **aftercare** — literally "care after" — self-care practice after an intense or emotionally charged experience [msg #157] · [Learning to rest](topics/rest/learning-to-rest.md)
- **allostatic load** — accumulated load on the nervous, hormonal and immune systems; the body functions but does not recover, producing weeks-long micro-symptoms [msg #476] · [Series: I'm 40 and worn out](topics/rest/tired-series.md)
- **BDNF** — brain-derived neurotrophic factor, described in the source as a neuron growth factor [msg #477] · [Fitness independence and sustainable movement](topics/body/fitness.md)
- **beauty burnout / Выгорание красотой** — exhaustion, anxiety and overload caused by constant pressure to meet beauty standards [msg #301] · [Body image, face and beauty](topics/body/beauty.md); [Women's fatigue and social expectations](topics/balance/womens-load.md)
- **brain body budget** — Barrett's metaphor: the brain as a financial director allocating the body's resources (glucose, oxygen, water); a budget deficit is felt as fatigue or stress [msg #70] · [Emotions as energy](topics/mind/emotions.md)
- **Brick** — small cube with an NFC tag that blocks chosen apps on the phone until the phone is tapped to it again [msg #328, #370] · [Digital hygiene and attention](topics/mind/digital-hygiene.md)
- **bulletproof-кофе** — black coffee blended in a shaker with MCT oil and ghee, drunk before breakfast [msg #16, #44] · [Energy boosters and replenishing practices](topics/energy/energy-boosters.md)
- **Contemplation / Размышление** — stage 2, ambivalence: you want change but fear it [msg #486] · [Why change is hard](topics/change/why-change-is-hard.md)
- **DASH-диета** — DASH diet; one of two diets the gastroenterologist calls research-confirmed [msg #151] · [Nutrition myths and evidence-based guidelines](topics/nutrition/nutrition-myths.md)
- **Default Mode Network (DMN)** — brain network active when we idle, daydream, do not plan or look at screens; tied to imagination, integrating experience, meaning, and the sense "I am in my place" [msg #448, #579] · [Series: I'm 40 and worn out](topics/rest/tired-series.md)
- **Default Mode Network (сеть пассивной работы мозга)** — the brain network active when a person is "doing nothing": replaying past scenes, rehearsing future dialogues, arguing with oneself [msg #360] · [Minimal effective dose of movement](topics/body/minimal-dose.md)
- **DRAMMA** — rest model: Detachment, Relaxation, Autonomy, Mastery, Meaning, Affiliation [msg #330] · [Learning to rest](topics/rest/learning-to-rest.md)
- **FOBO (fear of better options)** — fear that a better option exists, which makes a person doubt decisions; clear values remove it [msg #139] · [Tools and reflection practices](topics/change/tools.md)
- **FOMO / JOMO** — fear of missing out vs joy of missing out (on the superfluous) [msg #431] · [«На балансе с…» interview series](topics/community/interviews.md)
- **Future 100** — annual trend report by the agency VML, whose wellness section the author analysed [msg #655] · [Wellness independence and industry critique](topics/industry/wellness-independence.md)
- **Ghee / Гхи / Ги** — clarified butter made by heating milk or butter above 100 °C; lactose-free; used in Ayurveda [msg #16, #22] · [Energy boosters and replenishing practices](topics/energy/energy-boosters.md)
- **greyscale режим** — greyscale (black-and-white) phone display mode used to remove bright stimuli [msg #312] · [Three questions about energy (mini-interviews)](topics/energy/energy-interviews.md)
- **IFS** — Internal Family Systems — a parts approach; Katya says it is "also about parts, but from a slightly different angle" than psychodrama and transactional analysis [msg #323, #528] · [Inner work: self-worth, shame, parts](topics/mind/inner-work.md)
- **IFS (Internal Family Systems)** — therapy approach that highlights different inner "parts" (critic, creator, etc.) [msg #208] · [«На балансе с…» interview series](topics/community/interviews.md)
- **indirect prompt injection** — hidden instructions embedded in a website (invisible to humans, read by AI) that steer a chatbot's recommendation [msg #650] · [Supplements: benefits and harms](topics/industry/supplements.md)
- **keep up with the Joneses** — the drive to match others' visible lifestyle; here, turning vacations into a "fair of vanity" [msg #337] · [Series: I'm 40 and worn out](topics/rest/tired-series.md)
- **lagom** — Swedish concept: sufficiency, "just right", moderation, optimality — about expediency, not abstinence or deficit [msg #54] · [What is balance (and is it real)](topics/balance/what-is-balance.md)
- **Life Blend** — Katya Pakhomova's concept of interweaving life spheres instead of separating them [msg #417, #427] · [«На балансе с…» interview series](topics/community/interviews.md)
- **Life Dinner** — a coaching practice: once a month, over dinner, partners share feelings and experience of the past month, give feedback and discuss mutual goals [msg #117] · [Tools and reflection practices](topics/change/tools.md)
- **lifestyle medicine** — approach in which changing lifestyle prevents, controls and even reverses disease [msg #152] · [What energy is and where it comes from](topics/energy/what-is-energy.md)
- **low-FODMAP диета** — diet excluding or reducing foods high in fermentable oligosaccharides, disaccharides and polyols, for a sensitive gut [msg #154] · [Nutrition myths and evidence-based guidelines](topics/nutrition/nutrition-myths.md)
- **Maintenance / Поддержание** — stage 5, behavior has become habitual, about 6 months [msg #490] · [Why change is hard](topics/change/why-change-is-hard.md)
- **MC4R, FTO** — the two main "obesity genes"; Katya carries two copies of each [msg #741] · [Eating disorder recovery, weight genetics and body acceptance](topics/body/weight-and-ed-recovery.md)
