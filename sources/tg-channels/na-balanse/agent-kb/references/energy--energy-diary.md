# A year of the energy diary

## What this covers

This topic follows a longitudinal self-tracking experiment on the channel. It starts with a guest post by Lena Movshina about six weeks of tracking energy in a calendar (Nov 2024), then Katya's two-week time diary (Dec 2024), and then Katya's energy diary kept through all of 2025, with quarterly reports in March, July and October, a year-end summary in January 2026 and a post about a slump in January 2026. Each report has its findings, what she changed, and what she still doesn't know. According to the source, it matters because "Нам часто кажется, что мы все про себя знаем, но это не так" (*We often think we know everything about ourselves, but that's not so*). Honest numbers and daily notes turn vague feelings ("I work too much", "I am weak", "I'm full of energy") into data that you can act on [msg #225, #246, #514].

## Core ideas

- **Counting brings calm and correction.** Lena found that she works plenty, and Katya found that she does not work "all the time" but is simply very tired. Both conclusions came from honest counting, not from feelings. The details are in *Traffic-light calendar* and *Two-week time diary audit* [msg #225, #246].
- **Fragmentation drains more than workload.** Task-switching, cramming things between meetings and checking chats wear Katya out more than the tasks themselves do. It is a finding she repeats from December 2024 through to January 2026 [msg #246, #290, #347, #459].
- **The energy scale is miscalibrated by high expectations.** Katya lives at a "medium charge" of 60-75%. She later realised that 70-80% *is* her 100%, and that you only see the real scale by comparison. The details are in *Recalibrating the energy scale* [msg #290, #347, #439, #514].
- **Thoughts and emotions are energy leaks.** Anxiety, guilt, self-pity, anticipatory suffering and loss of meaning take more energy than lack of sleep or physical load [msg #290, #297, #351, #439, #514].
- **Energy capacity can grow** if the basics (sleep, food, quiet, rituals) are in place and you keep a "research mode" of noticing where you sag. The details are in *Energy capacity grows like a muscle* [msg #347, #514].
- **Effects are delayed.** A dip comes 1-2 days after the load, and stress can arrive 3-4 weeks later [msg #290, #514].
- **Slumps are normal. The skill is noticing them early.** The goal is not "never relapse" but to notice the откат sooner and recover. The details are in *Slump (откат) recovery protocol* [msg #520].

## Frameworks, models and step-by-step methods

### Traffic-light calendar (pacing "светофор" method, digital version) — Lena Movshina
**Background and purpose.** Guest author Lena Movshina had for years heard an anxious inner voice: "Ты недостаточно стабильная и выносливая, ты слабая, ты делаешь мало" (*You're not stable and resilient enough, you're weak, you do too little*). Neither years of therapy nor success at work fully helped; she performed well, but any calm moment could turn into an "anxious hell", and she was even diagnosed with an anxiety disorder. A year before the post she learned she has autism (РАС) and ADHD (СДВГ). While researching how to live with this she learned about pacing, "систему менеджемента энергии, которая помогает распределять силы равномернее и сохранять предсказуемый темп работы" (*an energy-management system that helps distribute effort more evenly and keep a predictable work pace*). She used the "traffic light" pacing method for 6 weeks, after first converting it into a digital version. Katya introduces the post by saying that, although Lena is neurodivergent, in Katya's view what Lena writes may be useful to anyone [msg #225].

**Steps:**
1. **Use Google Calendar as the tracking surface.** Mark time slots in different colours [msg #225, img #225].
2. **Colour-code each slot.** Mark energy spending in red, energy replenishment in green, and everything else in yellow. The aim is to "assemble a traffic light" every day [msg #225].
3. **Record after the fact, not as a plan.** Lena did not plan work and rest in advance. She marked hours "по факту" (*as they actually happened*) so she could study her *natural* pace [msg #225].
4. **Ask three research questions of the data.** How much do I really work? Do I set aside time for rest, or skip it? How pronounced is my "takeoff-crash" (взлет-падение) cycle? [msg #225]
5. **Label slots in plain, honest language.** The screenshot of her calendar for a week in Aug-Sep 2024 shows entries such as "ворк" (*work*), "сплю" (*sleeping*), "существую в благодати" (*existing in grace*), "стресс", "подустала" (*got a bit tired*), "откисаю" (*recovering/soaking*), "отмокаю в ванне" (*soaking in the bath*), "вечер с пиццей теликом и книжкой" (*evening with pizza, TV and a book*), "ворк в мыле" (*working in a lather*), plus calls, a massage and surfing. Each has a time range [img #225].

**Results Lena reports:**
1. **She learned how many hours a week she can really work.** It was "не мало" (*not little*), and she "fundamentally calmed down": "Против цифр не попрешь" (*You can't argue with numbers*). Her bands are: 12-20 hours a week is spread across the week almost unnoticeably. 30 hours is comfortably intensive. 40 effective hours is super-intensive. 50 effective hours a week in hyperfocus is her threshold, which she can sustain for about a week if a break follows [msg #225].
2. **She planned a freelance earning schedule** based on the hours she actually has. Before, she feared that counting hours would show *zero* and she would be "exposed". "Честный подсчет потребовал смелости, но и дал много уверенности" (*Honest counting took courage, but also gave a lot of confidence*) [msg #225].
3. **She came to respect her natural pace.** She really does need relaxed weeks and ASAP weeks. Demanding evenness from herself is uncomfortable, and she no longer thinks she has to be even [msg #225].

**Verdict and extensions.** Lena gives energy tracking 10/10. She notes that a calendar can be used to study other things too. She previously used it to measure how much time she spends mindlessly zoning out, and what time of day she prefers to work. Katya recommends the "calendar experiment" to anyone with a feeling of endless inadequacy [msg #225].

### Two-week time diary audit with category breakdown and next steps — Katya, Dec 2024
**Purpose.** Katya had tried this several times without success. She did it now because she wanted to know how "balanced" she really was, and because her 2025 goal was to learn to earn money. That meant figuring out *when* to do it, given that "сейчас и так времени не хватает примерно ни на что" (*right now there's already time for roughly nothing*) [msg #246].

**Method:**
1. **Pick two typical weeks.** [msg #246]
2. **On working days, write everything you do into a note from 6:00 to 18:00-19:00.** [msg #246]
3. **Categorise and compute shares.** Her categories were Self-care, Food & cooking, Social media, and Work, with Work split into Content, Clients and Development. She quotes ChatGPT's commentary on the breakdown, which suggests she used ChatGPT for the analysis [msg #246, img #248].
4. **For each finding, write a "Next steps" list.** [msg #246]

**Her breakdown** (card titled "На что я тратила время в 2024" (*What I spent time on in 2024*)) [img #248]:
- Self-care: 24.50% (20.5 h). This counts only mindfulness practices, sport and walks, not sleep, showering or eating.
- Food and cooking: 18.15% (15 h).
- Social media: 10.23% (9 h).
- Work: 46% (39 h), made up of Content 17.91% (14.97 h), Clients 19.71% (16.47 h) and Development 8.50% (7.10 h).

**The five findings and their next steps:**
1. **"Была уверена, что очень много работаю. Оказалось, что просто очень сильно устаю."** (*I was sure I work a lot. It turned out I'm just very tired.*) Time splits roughly evenly: work 46%, everything else 54%. She used to fantasise that working 4 hours a day would be great, and it turned out she works on average 4 h 10 min a day. The feeling of working all the time comes from how work is spread through the day, the urge to "cram in the uncrammable", and jumping between tasks. *Next steps:* experiment with the schedule, don't stuff everything into one day, and give different days to different tasks. She has long wanted to try Tim Ferriss's method [msg #246, img #247].
2. **Time spent on herself is more than time spent on clients or on creating content.** Self-care at 24.5% is the largest category. She quotes ChatGPT: "Это положительный тренд, свидетельствующий о том, что вы понимаете важность восстановления" (*This is a positive trend showing you understand the importance of recovery*). Lesson: when she feels like pitying herself (which she calls her "psychological Everest for 2025"), some self-pity is fine, but she should not let "toxic pity" take over, because she spends a lot of time on self-care. *Next steps:* work on self-pity in therapy. If it feels like "I'm working too much", review the schedule and the ways she recovers. "похоже, дело не в количестве, а в качестве" (*it seems it's not about quantity but quality*) [msg #246].
3. **More time goes to social-media scrolling than to development, finances and study.** It is about 9 hours a week, which she extrapolates to 34 hours a month and 408 hours (18 days) a year. *Next steps:* build social-media hygiene (she already doesn't read it in the mornings), set a separate time for reading chats and checking DMs, and stop "polishing" every activity with a visit to social media [msg #246].
4. **Very little time goes to development, and she procrastinates through routine work.** Development means thinking up new projects and building a site, bot or infrastructure, and it gets 8.5%. She regularly procrastinates with "useful actions", namely writing content; this very post was written instead of making an offer for online sales. Content takes 18% and, she says, leads nowhere in terms of the financial results that matter to her now. *Next steps:* raise development time to 15%, ideally 20%, in 2025. That needs work on mindset as well as planning: learn to think about development, wake up with those thoughts, not fear and not doubt. For this she went to a coach and resumed therapy [msg #246].
5. **"еда — моя вторая работа"** (*food is my second job*) is no joke. Cooking and eating take 18%, or 15 hours a week, which she puts at "30 days a year". *Next steps:* keep optimising through meal prep. When she first started cooking 1.5 years earlier, the first time in her life, it took 1.5-2 times longer and she "super-suffered" [msg #246].

**Headline lesson.** The title says she "achieved the ideal work/rest balance, but it turned out that isn't what I need". The roughly 50/50 split did not stop her from feeling overloaded, so the problem was distribution, switching and quality of recovery rather than quantity [msg #246, img #246, img #247].

### Energy diary with ChatGPT analysis prompt — Katya, from early 2025
**Why she started.** 2025 had two focuses, work and recovery. In January she gained five new clients. In February she added study in English (coaching in English twice a week). Calls multiplied, which she links to "Zoom-fatigue", and she started an energy diary. A photo of her on a Zoom call illustrates the post. By March she had her first observations [msg #290, img #290].

**How the diary is analysed.** Katya gives ChatGPT the diary with this prompt [img #291]:
- "Проанализируй мой дневник энергии:" (*Analyse my energy diary:*)
- "какие видишь тренды?" (*what trends do you see?*)
- "что влияет на спад энергии?" (*what causes energy dips?*)
- "что помогает поднять энергию?" (*what helps raise energy?*)
- "какие основные инсайты я выносила (с прямыми цитатами)" (*what main insights did I draw, with direct quotes*)
- "что вообще можно сказать о моей энергии за последний месяц?" (*what can be said overall about my energy over the last month?*)

**Diary entries Katya shared as examples (Q1)**, each showing the kind of observation the diary captures:
- *Rushing is not caused by calls.* "Поняла, что чувство спешки НЕ из-за звонков, а из-за того, что переживаю, что не успеваю «другое» - и пытаюсь засовывать что-то между." (*I realised the feeling of rush is NOT from the calls, but from worrying I'm not managing "other things" and trying to squeeze something in between.*) Proposed decision: when there are many calls in a row, do nothing else [img #292].
- *Not giving in to anxiety at night.* She woke up in anxiety right after falling asleep and put herself back to sleep with a book. She is proud that she doesn't follow the anxiety and doesn't turn on TV series at night [img #293].
- *Switching again.* "Опять скакала между задачами и чатами." (*Again jumped between tasks and chats.*) [img #294]
- *Stories as fake activity.* She noticed that on a low charge she starts wanting to post many Instagram stories, "Как будто это имитация деятельности, надо что-то кому-то доказывать" (*As if it's an imitation of activity, a need to prove something to someone*) [img #295].
- *Fear as a constant.* In a Kundalini session she strongly felt fear, first about clients who might not come, then about study where she must practise in English. "Как будто страх - моя естественная реакция на все. Наверное, это очень по-человечески." (*As if fear is my natural reaction to everything. Probably that's very human.*) [img #296]
- *Self-pity versus resisting it.* She realised "for the hundredth time" that a lot of energy goes into self-pity *and* into resisting it. All week she felt "poor and miserable" but gave herself no break, and "Это противостояние не дает делать вообще ничего" (*This standoff makes it impossible to do anything at all*) [img #297].

### Ten unexpected energy-diary findings (Q1 2025)
Katya deliberately leaves out the obvious ones, such as "less sleep, lower energy" or "low energy coincides with an emotional dip". She stresses that knowing something and *feeling it in yourself and starting to change* are different things. Main insight: "**Я** **не просто устаю**, а **живу в состоянии среднего заряда** — **обычно 60-75%**" (*I'm not just tired, I live in a state of medium charge, usually 60-75%*). She used to think she was bursting with energy, but the diary showed otherwise. The ten findings [msg #290]:
1. She doesn't jump up full of energy in the morning. It is often hard at first, but morning rituals energise her.
2. It is not the tasks that tire her but the constant switching between them.
3. When a list of "musts" (надо) piles up, procrastination in social media and an energy drop begin.
4. If she doesn't insert tasks between meetings, energy holds better.
5. One comment on social media can affect her concentration for several hours.
6. The dip comes not at the moment of load but 1-2 days later.
7. Anxiety and overload take more energy than lack of sleep.
8. Guilt ("я должна больше", *I should do more*) drains more than tiredness does. When she lets herself rest, for example lying around with a book, energy rises on its own.
9. Unfinished tasks drain energy even when she isn't working on them.
10. Even when checking chats and social media seems harmless, 30 minutes later she is scattered, and the more often she checks social media, the more her energy jumps around.

**Open questions she set herself:** Does she not know how to rest fully? Or has she not yet adapted to the load? Is she draining energy mindlessly? Or does she hold a belief, "я не могу долго быть в ресурсе" (*I can't stay resourced for long*), that keeps her body in the middle range? She also noticed that her tiredness comes in very different kinds, which show up differently and need different recovery. She planned to explore this the following month [msg #290].

### Energy changes that worked (Q2 2025)
In July Katya reported mid-year highlights under the title "«Мудрость в отношении энергии — знать, когда перестать в себя пихать»" (*Wisdom about energy is knowing when to stop stuffing things into yourself*) [msg #347]:
1. **Hearing the body earlier.** "Тело будто заговорило понятнее — или я научилась слушать." (*The body seems to speak more clearly, or I've learned to listen.*) She now senses an approaching dip in advance and can catch and soften it. "Lying around and zoning out" is now needed for at most a day or two, where before it stretched to a week.
2. **Recalibrating 100%.** This is set out in *Recalibrating the energy scale*.
3. **Doing less, getting the same.** Fuss and constant switching between small tasks were eating her battery. She cut her daily plan in half and her results did not drop. Instead, pauses of silence appeared. 5-15 minutes with closed eyes, or massage glasses between calls, give more than imitating vigorous activity or chaotic scrolling.
4. **Almost no "musts" in the calendar.** Some tasks could simply be crossed out, and the rest she reframed ("I'm a coach, after all"). Cooking and cleaning turned from bondage into "выдох и поделать что-то руками" (*an exhale and doing something with my hands*) between Zooms, and "learn to cook deliciously" even appeared in her 20-year strategy.
5. **Best call-free day: Wednesday.** Five days of 3-5 calls each was obviously "suicide". She first tried a "methodical Monday", which didn't help, because by Thursday she was squeezed out. A free Wednesday gives a breather mid-week and a sense that work other than client calls keeps moving.
6. **Less content, more birding.** In spring content physically nauseated her: she would switch on a podcast and couldn't listen, or open a post and couldn't read. She compares it to cigarettes, where lighting up brought not pleasure but self-disgust and "зачем ты это с собой делаешь, Катя?" (*why are you doing this to yourself, Katya?*). She unsubscribed from almost all podcasts and Telegram channels (Instagram was already cleaned). Nothing collapsed, and room appeared for walks with binoculars (birdwatching) and inspiration.

**Why she knows it works:** her energy capacity and "Zoom tolerance" grew. What seemed impossible in January, 3-5 meetings a day, became normal, and she did not burn out and even feels good. "Кажется, «мышцы энергии» действительно прокачиваются." (*It seems "energy muscles" really can be trained.*) [msg #347]

**Diary quotes she shared from Q2:**
- She keeps trying to cram far more into her days and weeks than is comfortable and is permanently in "hyper-productivity" mode, while she wants to be calm most of the time. She notes this also depends on goal-setting and asks herself: "Почему я все время недовольна? Почему мне все время недостаточно?" (*Why am I always dissatisfied? Why is it never enough for me?*) [img #347]
- "Делать то, что нужно и важно. И не переживать по поводу скорости." (*Do what's needed and important. And don't worry about speed.*) The expectation that she must do everything fast and all at once is very draining and devalues what is worth doing. She wants to remember the principle "тише едешь, дальше будешь" (*slow and steady wins the race*, literally "the quieter you go, the further you get") [img #348].
- No content gets in any more; it is "coming out of my ears", and it seems there is "nothing interesting" [img #349].
- "My time" or "time for myself" should be without the phone. It sounds trite, but over the last week she really felt it, to the point of not even wanting to pick up the phone [img #350].
- "Страдать заранее - плохая идея." (*Suffering in advance is a bad idea.*) More than once she has pitied herself in the morning and suffered, and then meetings got cancelled and the day turned out completely different [img #351].

### Recalibrating the energy scale (real 100%)
This is a model Katya built over the year about how to read your own charge:
1. **Start by measuring, not assuming.** In March the diary showed she lives at 60-75%, although she had believed she was bursting with energy [msg #290].
2. **Notice that your "norm" is inflated by expectations.** For a couple of months she calibrated wrongly: "норма" felt like fireworks at 150%. She recognised this as her constant life companion, inflated expectations. "**70–80 % — и есть мои 100 %.**" (*70-80% is my 100%.*) [msg #347]
3. **Define a realistic 100% by concrete markers:** a clear head, lightness in the body, no urge to collapse on the sofa, and inner resilience to triggers [msg #347].
4. **Re-read the scale in hindsight.** In the stressful Q3 she saw that what she had called "80%" earlier was actually the full 100. "Поняла, что настоящую шкалу энергии осознаешь только в сравнении." (*I realised you only grasp the real energy scale by comparison.*) In autumn she was in the "yellow zone", but without resistance or breakdowns [msg #439].
5. **Stop waiting for a fountain.** In the year-end summary: "мои реальные 100 % — это не фонтан, а в лучшем случае ручей с более ли менее стабильным напором" (*my real 100% is not a fountain but at best a stream with a more or less stable flow*). She no longer waits for a surge of inspiration to do tasks, support clients, write posts or be with loved ones. A basic minimum is enough, and she can run on it for a very long time. "Это понижение ожиданий от себя и своей энергии, парадоксально, дает огромное количество энергии." (*This lowering of expectations of myself and my energy, paradoxically, gives a huge amount of energy.*) [msg #514]

### Four horsemen of the energy apocalypse
**Origin.** Katya's client Sveta Shedina named these in a video. The phrase "добываем энергию, как нефть" (*we extract energy like oil*) is what the channel does too. Katya uses the Welltory app and tracks on her watch how her energy drains. Her photo shows a watch "BATTERY" graph falling from about 100 at a 5:26 AM wake-up to 49% by 2:56 on a Wednesday. Energy does not drain evenly: some actions conserve charge during the day, and some burn it out completely, sometimes before lunch [msg #459, img #459].

**Sveta's four horsemen:**
1. Multitasking.
2. Ignoring your emotional states.
3. Ignoring mini-rests.
4. "Впихивание невпихуемого" (*cramming in the uncrammable*).

**Katya's own top list:** numbers 3 and 4, plus back-to-back Zoom meetings, information noise disguised as a search for inspiration, and rushing. "**Если что-то из этого происходит, значит всадники несут меня на энергетическое дно.**" (*If any of this is happening, the horsemen are carrying me to the energy bottom.*) She will most likely not feel rested that evening, or even the next day. She invites readers to name their own horsemen [msg #459].

### Slump (откат) recovery protocol — January 2026
**Context.** In January 2026 Katya again failed to notice she had drained herself. She calls it an oxymoron to burn out while running a group called «Отдохнувшие» (*"The Rested"*), as funny as getting stress (stomach pain) from "Magnesium anti-stress". Contributing factors: winter, the stress of moving, the stress of a new project, and atmospheric pressure in Moscow. She had not felt high pressure for five years and now lies flat every week [msg #520].

**Why it happened: the load she added without noticing**, alongside the joy, meaning and drive of the new project [msg #520]:
- She took away her full day off. A Saturday call of 2-2.5 hours plus preparation makes a working day.
- She added being present in the group chat, and had not read so many messages so thoughtfully in a long time.
- She started reworking and reassembling the programme "on the fly", because she wants content that is relevant, not content for its own sake.
- Plus "the classic set": doubts, anxiety, responsibility and self-flagellation over "am I giving enough".

**Detection.** By mid-January she found herself burnt out. Attention to her energy and continuous diary-keeping, which she still does and shares with the group, helped her see the problem quickly [msg #520].

**What she did once she saw the slump:**
1. She took her day off back: until the end of February, Thursday daytime is free.
2. She strengthened recovery with banya, massage and rituals, including a massage in the banya for the first time.
3. She stopped scheduling social meetings straight after big work meetings.
4. She again made time alone with herself a priority.
5. She planned a mini-study of how much energy goes into chats.

**Framing.** Through the lens of the theory of change she described in December, she was simply at a logical stage of any change: the rollback (откат), which happens to everyone at every stage. "**Фокус тут не в том, чтобы «никогда не откатываться» — это утопия. Фокус в навыке раньше замечать, что ты в откате.**" (*The focus isn't on "never rolling back", that's a utopia. The focus is the skill of noticing earlier that you're in a rollback.*) The trap to avoid is getting stuck in the familiar pit of "господи, у меня опять не получилось - какая я никчемная - сколько можно" (*God, I failed again, how worthless I am, how much longer*). "Отряхнулась — и пошла дальше." (*Dusted myself off and moved on.*) The adult version of change, in her words, is not "I never fall" but "я быстрее понимаю, что упала, и знаю, как встать" (*I realise faster that I've fallen, and I know how to get up*). Repetition is the mother of learning, and each cycle makes you a little less harsh with yourself and a little more attentive [msg #520].

## Detailed insights

### Q3 2025: anxiety as the dominant energy drain
- **The context.** July 2025 was her peak of stability and calm for the year, and she recorded a podcast about it. By the end of July her husband had lost his job and a new emigration was looming. "Если говорить по Маслоу — у моей пирамиды выбили фундамент." (*In Maslow's terms, the foundation of my pyramid was knocked out.*) She learned about her reactions and how much she still has to grow, especially in emotional resilience and trust in life [msg #439].
- **"Я уже и забыла, сколько энергии жрет тревога."** (*I had already forgotten how much energy anxiety eats.*) The brain confuses uncertainty with danger. Even when floating or walking she could not relax, because the inner "Tales from the Crypt" switched on: the critic and the controller. A diary entry reads: "Из меня херачит стресс. Очень сложно не фиксироваться на “что будет”. Сложно признать, что я узнаю это только тогда, когда это произойдет." (*Stress is pouring out of me. It's very hard not to fixate on "what will happen". Hard to admit I'll only find out when it happens.*) She cried, couldn't sleep, had crazy dreams, and woke at 5:30. The app said she slept fine, at 86, and she went for a run [msg #439].
- **Self-added uncertainty.** In therapy she realised that, on top of the external uncertainty, she was adding her own: she hired an assistant, was preparing new products, and cut client hours. "Все это прыжки веры, и страшно. Но, кажется, удачных моментов не бывает." (*All these are leaps of faith, and it's scary. But it seems there are no good moments.*) [msg #439]
- **Sleep, caffeine, supplements** (she labels this "не является врачебной рекомендацией", *not medical advice*). Autumn needs more sleep, which she already knew, and with anxiety sleep simply "steps out for a smoke", something she hadn't experienced for 1.5 years. Diary: "Просыпаюсь ночью, колбасит, в горле ком, в голове каша, не могу спать…" (*I wake at night, I'm shaking, lump in my throat, mush in my head, can't sleep…*). Under this stress she had for some reason stopped taking magnesium and omega, and her sleep fell fully into the red zone. Restarting the supplements brought RHR and HRV back to normal and sleep back to the green zone. On caffeine: under anxiety it "literally throws fuel on the fire". After a few sips her heart pounds, her chest tightens and breathing gets hard. She cut the dose from 12 to 4 grams and now has no "special effects" [msg #439].
- **Less energy means less inspiration, but routines keep her afloat.** She runs, writes and reads serious books less. Anxiety pushes her toward escapism: detective novels instead of philosophy (for the first time in years) and series instead of silence. These are at least safe forms of escape, but she sees her growth point as "не убегать, а смотреть тревоге в глаза" (*not running away but looking anxiety in the eye*). The routines that hold even without inspiration are morning exercise, meditation, a walk, floating, normal food and intervals of silence. These, she says, have kept her resourced for two months and moving forward confidently [msg #439].
- **Perspective.** Things that worried her in January, such as "the number of calls", are now out of the picture. She wants to be wiser, look at herself from the future, and remember the mantra "Так будет не всегда" (*It won't always be like this*). She is also fully back in therapy [msg #439].
- **Value of keeping the diary all year.** She is happy she kept it all year: "вижу настоящую перспективу, а не только сиюминутные колебания. Становится меньше иллюзий, больше ясности." (*I see the real perspective, not just momentary fluctuations. Fewer illusions, more clarity.*) [msg #439]

### Energy capacity grows like a muscle (year-end conclusion)
A year earlier she suddenly became in demand, with 5 new clients in January, and she was literally shaking at the thought of two meetings a day. It seemed a ceiling beyond which lay rollback, tears and "I can't do this any more". Now she has days with four meetings, which she says she does *not* recommend, plus project sales, new product development, and preparing podcasts and talks in parallel: "Это много. Это правда много. Но я не умерла. Не сломалась. Не лежу пластом." (*It's a lot. It really is a lot. But I didn't die. Didn't break. I'm not lying flat.*) Now completely different volumes scare her. **Condition:** if your base is built (sleep, food, quiet, rituals) and research mode is on, meaning you keep a hand on the pulse and notice in time where you are sagging, you can carefully go beyond your usual limits and train your energy like muscles. She had written this idea years earlier when founding the channel as "a pretty POV". Now it is "прожитой факт" (*a lived fact*) [msg #514].

### Self-limitation through thoughts (year-end conclusion)
She has repeatedly caught herself de-energising herself in advance. If she thinks in the morning "Oh God, four meetings today", energy is often gone by 9 a.m. If she goes into the day without expectations, drama or a script of "how hard this will be", she often ends the day in the plus. "Мысли — это не просто мысли. Это прямые утечки энергии." (*Thoughts aren't just thoughts. They are direct energy leaks.*) She now tries to tune into each day neutrally, with the reminder "**я. не знаю. как. пройдет. день. и. как. мне. будет.**" (*I. don't. know. how. the. day. will. go. or. how. I'll. feel.*) This builds on the Q2 diary note "Suffering in advance is a bad idea" [msg #514, img #351].

### Further year-end findings
- Emotions and loss of meaning knock her out worse than physical load [msg #514].
- Seasonality, PMS and the full moon are "не эзотерика, а реальные циклы" (*not esotericism but real cycles*) that affect her energy [msg #514].
- Effects reach her with a delay. Sleep deprivation, too much socialising or a quarrel show up only after a few days, and stress after as much as 3-4 weeks [msg #514].
- What restores her best is time alone with herself [msg #514].
- After the slump: "если выстроить отношения с нагрузкой, восстановлением, режимом, — энергии больше, чем кажется. Просто она не бесконечная и не «бесплатная»" (*if you build a relationship with load, recovery and routine, there's more energy than it seems. It's just not infinite and not "free"*) [msg #520].

### Culture of admitting fatigue
Katya says the hardest part of a slump is admitting it, to the group, to readers and above all to herself: that she has less energy now and is not all-powerful or perfect. Inside lives the myth that if you are "about resources, resilience, mindfulness" you must always be in perfect shape, and even more so as a woman, compared with the "girls with perfect hair and heels" on Instagram. Her stated aim: "**я хочу поддерживать культуру, где не стыдно сказать: «мне тяжело», «я устала», «мне нужен отдых»**" (*I want to support a culture where it's not shameful to say "it's hard", "I'm tired", "I need rest"*), where you don't have to pretend to be superhuman to have the right to a pause. "Если вы сейчас в похожем месте, вы не сломались. Вы просто живете." (*If you're in a similar place now, you haven't broken. You're just living.*) [msg #520]

## Practical playbook

- **Social-media hygiene** (from the time audit): don't read social media in the mornings, set a separate time for chats and DMs, and don't "polish" each task with a social-media check [msg #246].
- **Call-heavy days:** don't fit other tasks between calls. Decide that on many-calls days you do nothing else [msg #290, img #292].
- **Weekly structure:** keep one call-free day mid-week (Wednesday worked; Monday didn't). During the January 2026 slump, a free Thursday daytime served the same purpose [msg #347, #520].
- **Micro-recovery between calls:** 5-15 minutes with eyes closed, or massage glasses [msg #347].
- **Halve the daily plan.** In her experience results stayed the same [msg #347].
- **Phone-free "my time"** [img #350].
- **Neutral morning framing** instead of anticipating a hard day [msg #514, img #351].
- **Don't schedule social meetings right after big work meetings** [msg #520].
- **When anxious:** keep the base routines going, watch caffeine (it amplifies anxiety symptoms), and don't drop supplements she was already taking. She frames all of this as personal experience, not medical advice [msg #439].

## Evidence and numbers

All figures are self-reported n=1 observations.
- Lena (6 weeks of calendar tracking): 12-20 h/week is barely noticeable, 30 h comfortable-intensive, 40 effective h super-intensive, 50 effective h/week in hyperfocus her threshold, sustainable for about a week with a break afterwards [msg #225].
- Katya's time audit: work 46% / other 54%. Work is about 4 h 10 min/day on average. Self-care 24.5%, food 18%, social media about 9 h/week, development 8.5% (target 15-20% in 2025), content 18% [msg #246, img #248].
- Q1 2025 energy level is usually 60-75%. Dips lag load by 1-2 days. Scattered attention follows 30 minutes after checking chats [msg #290].
- Q2: recovery downtime shortened from about a week to 1-2 days. Zoom load grew from "impossible" to a normal 3-5 meetings/day [msg #347].
- Q3: sleep score 86 on a night of crying and crazy dreams. Caffeine cut from 12 to 4 grams. After restarting magnesium and omega, RHR and HRV returned to normal [msg #439].
- Year-end: from fearing 2 meetings/day to days of 4 meetings. Stress effects arrive 3-4 weeks later [msg #514].
