# Recommendations: channels, podcasts, books, series

## What this covers

This topic gathers every curated recommendation Katya Strelnikova made on the channel: the "На кого подписаться" ("Whom to follow") rubric of small Telegram channels (#читаем_набалансе), a podcast round-up (#слушаем_набалансе), a list of TV series about women, a health-habits interview, and two posts recommending six courses (#рекомендуем_набалансе). For each resource the file records what it is about, whom Katya thinks it suits, and her personal verdict, plus the transferable ideas she lifted from them. Katya presents these as resources she herself reads, listens to or has taken; the rubric is explicitly based on mutual post exchanges with the channels, which readers should keep in mind when weighing the praise [msg #107, #120].

## Core ideas

- **Small, soulful channels are worth following.** Katya calls herself a fan of small channels and shares ones that are made "с душой" (with soul); she chooses channels where she recognises herself in the introduction post — shared themes like burnout, self-search, coaching or balance [msg #107, #156, #181].
- **Rest must include mental rest.** The burnout channel she recommends teaches that you can burn out even from work you love, and that rest means not only doing something unrelated to work but also not thinking about it [msg #173, #174].
- **"Full version" instead of "best version" of yourself.** From the Doronichev interview, Katya adopts the idea of striving for a full rather than a best version of oneself, because the "best version" and achievement framing started to grate on her in the health sphere too [msg #580].
- **Every change has a stall phase.** The career-change recommendation describes a typical pattern: initial energy, then a phase with no results yet where the new path is not yet a support, when people doubt and quit [msg #648].
- **Interviews widen the view of roles and strategies.** Katya says listening to interviews with women helps her broaden her view of roles, life goals and strategies [msg #189].

## Frameworks, models and step-by-step methods

### Five reminders of the "Чтобы не выгорать" channel
Katya recommends the channel "Чтобы не выгорать" (@notburningout), which posts short, dense posts that can be applied in practice daily — ideas on how to work, rest and cope ("вывозить"). For her it acts as a daily reminder of what matters: some posts push her to act or help with change, some work "как терапия" (like therapy), and weekend memes go straight to her husband. She lists what you will not forget once subscribed [msg #173]:
1. **Balance between work and rest** — keep both in the picture rather than letting work swallow rest [msg #173].
2. **We work to live, not live to work** — work is a means for life, not its purpose [msg #173].
3. **Uncertainty is not a reason to stand still (even though it is scary)** — fear of the unknown should not freeze movement [msg #173].
4. **Everyone has their own path, and everyone makes mistakes** — comparison and perfectionism are dropped [msg #173].
5. **Praise yourself and notice small steps** — progress is acknowledged at the level of small actions [msg #173].

Example posts from the channel, shown as screenshots:
- "Remember that if you work non-stop, you can get tired and burn out even from the work you love most. Don't forget to rest even if you really like your work. After rest you'll miss it and return with new strength and new motivation" — "устать и выгореть можно даже от самого любимого дела" [img #173].
- "Observe what you think about during rest. Remember that when you rest, it is important not only to do something unrelated to work but also to not think about work" [img #174].
- A meme: "When I come home after a hard day I take a shower and then lie down in a warm bed — what a beautiful life after all", captioned "Не забывайте заботиться о себе так, как вам подходит и нравится" (Don't forget to take care of yourself in the way that suits you and that you like) [img #175].

Katya adds that she has long wanted to start a channel in a similar short format but "the long genre won't let go yet" [msg #173].

### Doronichev's no-shortcuts health list (and Katya's five takeaways)
Katya (calling herself the "main slowpoke") listened late to an interview with Andrey Doronichev about healthy habits. What resonated most was the idea of the "полная версия себя" (full version of yourself): she realised the thought of a "best version" and achievements had begun to grate on her in health too, and the "full version" is the person she wants to be and strive towards [msg #580].

Doronichev's own summary (screenshot, "if you're too lazy to watch, in short"): there are no shortcuts. The list, item by item [img #580]:
1. Fix your sleep.
2. Get up at dawn.
3. Do yoga.
4. Get into an ice-cold shower.
5. Eat fewer calories.
6. More protein and greens.
7. Drink water.
8. Don't drink alcohol.
9. Don't smoke and don't use drugs ("не долби").
10. Walk.
11. Dance and sing.
12. Do an hour of sport every day.
13. Sign up with a therapist.
14. Meditate.
15. Find someone to love.
16. And a cause to burn for.
17. Read long books.
18. Stop zoning out on your phone.

What pleased Katya most (her own selection) [msg #580]:
1. **The main focus and experiments are about sleep** — sleep is the priority lever.
2. **Find your own setup of useful habits** that make you feel good ("по кайфу") — not a universal regime.
3. **Don't stuff yourself with unnecessary chemistry** — even if it is food, and especially Ozempic; note the word "лишнюю" (excess/unnecessary) is emphasised.
4. **Go with friends not to a bar but to yoga or a sauna** (and surfing, "but not all of us are so lucky").
5. **You are not an ambulance** — you don't have to live with notifications on and random calls.

Her P.S.: she is glad her healthy lifestyle brings her no less pleasure and energy; when she currently lacks energy, she knows "это всегда про смыслы" (it is always about meaning) [msg #580].

### The stall phase of career change
Taken from Katya's description of Olya Polishchuk's webinar "Измениться и не слиться" (Change and don't drop out). Katya says this is a phenomenon she constantly sees in her lifestyle work [msg #648]:
1. **Start** — we begin career changes: start a blog, change the form of employment, take first steps.
2. **Energy surge** — energy appears, a feeling "наконец сдвинулось" (finally it's moving).
3. **Breakdown** — then everything breaks: there are no results yet, and the new path has not yet become a support.
4. **Doubt** — questions start: "А точно ли я туда иду?" (Am I really going the right way?), "Может, у меня в другом лучше получится?" (Maybe I'd do better at something else?).
5. **Freeze and quit** — we freeze and can easily abandon the idea.
The webinar promises to cover where the line runs between "this isn't for me" and the normal course of any change, why discipline and planning stop working at some point, and how to still bring things to a result. (!) INCOMPLETE: the source describes the webinar's questions but not its answers [msg #648].

### Who "Средненький продакт" suits (four reader profiles)
Katya recommends Anton's channel "Средненький продакт" ("Mediocre product [manager]"), which became especially interesting to her after he left employment and now, like other self-employed "rank-and-file workers", runs into limits of calendar and resource. She reads it like a series — she always wants to know what happens next. It suits those [msg #289]:
1. whose work-life balance looks more like a "work-life mess";
2. who want to level up their productivity but without toxic "getting things done";
3. who like reading about work and life without illusions and "success at any cost";
4. who sometimes lie in bed scrolling shorts and shed a "manly tear" (Anton did too, but found an alternative).
Posts she especially liked: "Work-life blend vs. классические системы продуктивности: 1:0" (work-life blend beats classic productivity systems), "Бэклог низкой энергии" (the low-energy backlog), "Умер на созвоне" (Died on a call), "Получаю дикое удовольствие от простых вещей" (I get wild pleasure from simple things), and one on his complicated relationship with cheap dopamine [msg #289].

## Detailed insights

### Small Telegram channels ("Плюс-минус 300" selection)
Katya says she is a fan of small channels — presumably her readers too, since they read her "(for now) modest herald of energy and balance". She shares several because they are made with soul (crossing out "mutual PR"), and asks readers for their own recommendations [msg #107]:
- **Маша пишет** — Marusya, a bath-house master ("банная мастерица"); Katya jokes she rarely meets a profession wilder than health coach and tips her hat. She writes about the history, traditions and rituals of bath culture and shares very personal discoveries and thoughts. Katya plans to attend her steaming session when in Moscow [msg #107].
- **Agence d'astrologie** — Sveta's astrology channel. Katya openly admits reading an astrologer, having had an astrological reading, a Human Design reading, tarot and metaphorical-card readings: "не отказываю себе ни в каких способах познать себя" (I don't deny myself any ways to know myself). She found Sveta in someone's round-up and stayed for the intimacy and warmth; Sveta analysed her chart on the theme of calling (Katya jokes that being a health coach and running a channel is written in the stars). The channel offers daily and monthly forecasts and explains what Mercury's position means for your sign [msg #107].
- **Зона комфорта** — Vika's channel, which Katya credits as the initiator of the whole rubric: after Vika's message they decided to talk about small great channels. Katya and Vika share an experience: both burned out and both moved to Asia — "Лучший план — только вообще не выгорать" (the best plan is simply not to burn out at all). The channel is about stress management for freelancers and entrepreneurs, because corporate employees have bosses and HR, "а у нас — только мы" (and we only have ourselves). It has many applied tools and research-based information about stress and handling it [msg #107].
- **superola** — Olya, with whom Katya worked in a PR agency over 10 years ago; life took them into health coaching and cooking. Olya bakes cakes, designs menus for trendy restaurants and "just eats tastily"; her channel shows how to make beautiful dishes from simple ingredients, what seasonal produce to buy at the market, where and what to eat, and looks for balance in cooking and beyond [msg #107].

### Slow living: Anya Fedorova's channel
"Эстетика Блаженства: Путешествие в Мир Радости и Разочарования" (Aesthetics of Bliss: A Journey into the World of Joy and Disappointment) is Anya Fedorova's channel; the unusual name was suggested by AI on the request "придумай название книги про счастье в стиле Набокова" (come up with a title for a book about happiness in Nabokov's style). In autumn 2023 Anya left employment after 18 years of a successful media career and now works as a coach, helping people overcome creative and personal blocks, become more creative and balance career and personal life. On the channel she explores the transition from a tense career race to Slow Living, which comes neither easily nor fast. As an ambassador of «лежуньканья» (lazy lying-around) she posts content for "seal days", simple salad recipes, how to bring creativity back into life, and speaks openly about money and her projects (e.g. a conversational English club) [msg #120].

Anya's own description of the channel: «Жизнь очень большая (исторические события, глобальные цели, мировые культурные веяния) и одновременно очень маленькая (салатик, хорошая книга, набросок в блокноте) и я люблю её разную.» — life is both very big and very small, and she loves it in all its forms; "slow living without hermitage, with full acceptance of the surrounding reality". Posts Katya highlights: what to do if you're tired at the start of the week; a financial plan for moving from employment to freelance; on leaving employment; caring for what you have; organising your wardrobe and decluttering it yourself [msg #120].

### Lifestyle and career channels
- **Dasha (beautifuleverything)** — breakfasts and marketing. Katya subscribed because in the intro post she saw *свою* (one of her own kind) — not because Dasha works in marketing, but because of her line «Я стремлюсь выстраивать такую жизнь, в которой и работе, и всему, что вне её, хочется и получается отдаваться на 1000%» (I strive to build a life in which I want to and manage to give 1000% both to work and to everything outside it). Katya: "Мы тоже!" The channel is something to "wrap yourself in like a blanket" and read instead of chatting with a girlfriend; it has breakfast spots, places for (beautiful) work and unexpected ones, Moscow, marketing, job vacancies and occasional important insights [msg #156].
- **Бесэдер (Anya)** — Katya subscribed after three code words in the intro: self-search, burnout and coaching ("Сестра, подумала я" — Sister, I thought). Unlike Katya, Anya burned out not in a full-time office job but after travelling the world with one backpack, finding hourly-paid remote work and accidentally becoming a slasher. Now a chief editor at Yandex, she "isn't even thinking of burning out" because she reflects, shares experience, doesn't overdo office sitting, and values mobility and travel. "Бесэдер" is Hebrew for "everything is fine". She writes about career, life, habits and self-search, all backed by personal experience, so it often resonates. Katya's favourite posts: "Идите домой" (Go home) — your overtime is needed by no one; "Эффект бабочки" — how one thing hooks onto another and you only need to start, which Katya calls "толкануть тачку" (push the cart); "Baby steps этого лета" — the power of small steps. Katya also remarks that Yandex is prolific in great people and channels [msg #181].
- **Институтка (Sasha)** — for "those who no longer want the old way but don't know the new way". Katya senses a wave of transformation around her, as if everyone simultaneously decided to change life, work and self, or at least find a direction — and she herself is searching for her path, mission and identity. She subscribed after an episode with Katya Keilman, initially for podcasts with inspiring women, and found lots of personal experience and transformation tools which she tries herself or saves for work with clients. Sasha and her partner Yulia run a four-week practicum-intensive "Кто мы? Откуда? Куда мы идем?" (Who are we? Where from? Where are we going?) for people stuck "в лимбе самоопределения" (in the limbo of self-determination) who want a new vision of themselves, new directions, and understanding of desires and goals [msg #189].

### Podcasts ("Услышано")
Katya shares four podcasts that touched her over a month, as a way of supporting the podcast trend without recording her own [msg #206]:
- **On Purpose (Jay Shetty) with Dr Joe Dispenza**, "5 Ways You're Blocking Yourself from Manifesting Love & 7 Steps to Attract Genuine Love" — framed as about what prevents us from feeling love, but really about how thoughts of the past control our future. Katya is a longtime fan; she read his book «Сила подсознания» twice (most recently this year, where it lands on her Kundalini practice "как в сухую землю" — like water into dry earth). At first his ideas seemed like pseudoscience to her, but her view of "science" has since changed and her notion of "how things really are" widened. (!) Her own hedge: she once considered it pseudoscience [msg #206].
- **Masha Revinskaya: why go to a coach, how to survive a failure and stop devaluing yourself** — a former The Village colleague who "fully opened up" for Katya in this episode. A very honest and clear conversation on managing great projects and big teams without strain ("без надрыва"), while finding time to lie on the sofa and hear yourself. Katya: the episode is literally an advertisement for coaching, and she signs under every word [msg #206].
- **"Тело привычки" (Mela brand) with trainer Ekaterina Khomenko**, "Принимать себя разным в моменте" — on healthy lifestyle, bodybuilding, strength training, pilates, nutrition and motivation. Katya finds it pleasant to hear an adequate person from her field; recommended if you are thinking about how to be healthy, start moving and not quit, or get into wellness (and yourself) [msg #206].
- **"Хорошие отношения" — «Как быть здоровой в современном мире?»** — an episode where Katya herself was the invited expert. She adores the podcast, discovered it relatively recently and is relistening to all episodes from the last 2.5 years: a good place to hear sound reasoning by adequate people on various topics. (Conflict of interest: she appears in it.) [msg #206]

### TV series about women for women
Katya's list, prompted by watching Riot Women and Ponies, noting that it is not only "Dying for Sex" (already widely covered) [msg #530]:
- **Riot Women / Бунтарки** — British series about women 50+ who no longer want to be convenient ("удобными"): age, friendship, fatigue, anger and freedom, with humour. She watched each episode thinking "Go, girl!", perhaps because she has recently started feeling herself a woman ("не девочкой, и не девушкой" — not a girl, not a young lady); she feels a vibe kinship with the British.
- **Ponies / Пони** — a light thriller-comedy about female identity, role, expectations and how we lock ourselves into them; set in Soviet Moscow (Zhiguli cars, Russians with American accents, scary KGB agents). It gripped her.
- **Bad Sisters / Заговор сестер Гарви** — about toxic relationships, loyalty, guilt and love that doesn't always look pretty; "Очень терапевтичный сериал" (a very therapeutic series) and really funny.
- **Three Women / Три женщины** — about how differently we experience sex, power, intimacy and shame; at times dramatic and painful, sometimes almost fantastical yet very true to life.
- **Physical / В ритме жизни** (2021) — about the body, control, eating disorders (РПП), fitness as religion and an inner voice that never goes quiet. Caveat: if you have a difficult relationship with your body it may trigger, but it can also illuminate a lot. Katya plans to rewatch it.
[msg #530]

### Courses (two posts, six courses)
Katya presents three courses she is not taking (she promised herself to concentrate on relocating for the first half of the year — her fifth move, so she knows how serious a stress it is) but wants to, then three more [msg #625, #648]:
- **Лаборатория развития эмоционального равновесия** (Emotional Balance Development Lab) by Anya Bichevskaya and Lobsang — about identifying and managing your emotions. Based on Paul and Eve Ekman's approach, combining scientific understanding of emotions as a natural human mechanism, neuroscience, deep psychological methods and mindfulness practices. Format: 9 weeks, weekly meetings, practices between meetings and a support group that remains indefinitely. Katya previously took their "Курс про счастье" (and says she became happier) and the "Лаборатория по философскому интеллекту" (where she found a theme she is developing this year) [msg #625].
- **Лаборатория удовольствия** (Pleasure Lab) by Sveta Shedina — about contact with what lights you up. Katya's reasoning: over recent years we have levelled up in many things and learned a lot, "но почему-то разучились хотеть и мечтать" (but for some reason forgot how to want and dream); she thinks most people she knows need it. 4 weeks, weekly, mini-group. Katya earlier took Sveta's "Аутентичная коммуникация" and "made friends with her parts" [msg #625].
- **Курс по развитию аутентичного блога** (Authentic blog course) by Olya Bastron — Katya took it herself and would go again. It does not teach "algorithms" and "reach strategies" but helps you understand why you write at all, what you want to say, and how to find your voice and style. 7 weeks: practicums, masterminds, group meetings, buddies. Katya was to give a live talk there about the mood for running a blog [msg #625].
- **Olya Polishchuk — webinar «Измениться и не слиться» and her lab «Перепридумай карьеру»** (Reinvent your career) — Olya is Katya's friend, a "career guide of all Russia"; Katya took the lab in 2020 and says if you are at a career crossroads nobody will advise you anything better. Webinar content is in *The stall phase of career change* above [msg #648].
- **«Неидеальный план» (Imperfect plan) by Zhenya Datsko** — a mentoring programme about gentle movement toward your goals: without hypercontrol, Napoleonic plans and deflating after a week; about stages of change, your own rhythm and how to return to movement when you have dropped out of the process. Good if you planned to change something over the summer and want to build your system of movement. Katya has not taken it, but many friends have and praise it [msg #648].
- **«Мой первый запуск» (My first launch) by Sasha Kolkina** — six weeks on taking an idea to a real product, from "why am I doing this" to first sales; for anyone with a long-held idea that never became a launch, to figure out why and start. Katya took Sasha's sales course last summer and afterwards launched her own product "Отдохнувшие", so she recommends it "не просто так" (not for nothing) [msg #648].

## Caveats, disagreements and open questions

- The "На кого подписаться" rubric features channels with which the authors "договорились обменяться постами друг о друге" (agreed to exchange posts about each other) — the recommendations are partly mutual promotion [msg #120, #156, #173, #181, #189, #289].
