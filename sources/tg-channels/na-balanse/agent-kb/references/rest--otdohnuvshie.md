# «Отдохнувшие»: the rest laboratory

## What this covers

This topic gathers everything the channel says about «Отдохнувшие» ("The Rested Ones"): Katya Strelnikova's personal essay that defined what being rested means to her, the closed peer group of seven women she ran for about ten months in 2025, what that group taught its members (in Katya's retrospective and in three member testimonials), the design and promised outcomes of the open six-week laboratory launched in January 2026, and two client case studies (Anya, Sasha) that show the rest methods applied in one-to-one coaching. [msg #280, #450, #465, #491, #495, #598, #628]

According to the source, this matters because rest is not a reward or a luxury but a skill that has to be planned, observed and built into real life; people who skip it run on "the last breath", notice fatigue only when they are already lying down sick, and cannot sustain any other change (work, food, weight) without it. [msg #280, #495, #598, #628]

## Core ideas

- **Rest is an activity that must be planned.** Katya defines rest as a process and activity aimed at restoring body, energy and resource, which happens only when it is competently planned. Full detail in *Rested-me essay*. [msg #280]
- **Rest is a skill, not a luxury or reward.** The laboratory's first promised outcome is the understanding that rest is "не роскошь и не награда, а навык, который можно развивать и встроить в жизнь" (not a luxury and not a reward but a skill that can be developed and built into life). Members (Radmila, Karina) independently report the same realisation. [msg #495, #466, #468]
- **Observation + people + regularity keep you on track.** Katya built the group because these three supports help her keep pace in other areas; weekly written check-ins made recurring drains impossible to "unsee". See *Observation, people, regularity* and *Weekly check-in diary*. [msg #450]
- **Experiments beat goals for rest.** Goals for rest fail when one's skill level is near zero because life and habits intervene; a hypothesis → test → change loop works better. See *Experimental approach*. [msg #450]
- **Energy is cyclical, for everyone.** The group's most valuable lesson for Katya was seeing energy cycles in others, which made slumps acceptable: they are unavoidable, harsher if you squeeze yourself, and happen to everyone. [msg #450, #467]
- **Rest more, tire less, work better.** The Anya case concludes: "больше отдыхаешь, меньше устаешь, больше эффективность" (you rest more, you tire less, efficiency is higher). Anya now does more in 5–6 hours than previously in 10. [msg #598]
- **The main result is a new contact with oneself; habits follow.** Katya's view is that the key outcome of rest work is not new habits but a new contact with oneself, after which habits appear on their own. [msg #598]
- **Work with the state, not the symptom.** Sasha came about food and weight and found that without rest there is no energy and without energy there is no change. [msg #628]

## Frameworks, models and step-by-step methods

### Rested-me essay — «Отдохнувшая. Я.» (11 markers of the rested state)
Purpose: an essay on rest was one of the tasks in the first month of the closed club; Katya published hers and invited readers to say what resonates and what she forgot. Each paragraph opens with the refrain "Отдохнувшая. Я." (Rested. Me.) and describes one condition under which she is rested. It works as a self-description template: a reader can write their own list of when they are rested. She notes (in a screenshot of her note) that she wrote it right after waking up on her third consecutive day off — "настолько преисполнилась к третьему выходному" (was that filled up by the third day off). [msg #280, img #281]

1. **Inspiration and desire.** Rested is when there is inspiration and energy to realise what was planned, time without hurry, and when she really "ХОООЧЕТСЯ" (waaants) to do things. [msg #280]
2. **Enough sleep.** When she has slept enough — gave herself as much sleep as needed, wakes early without an alarm, falls asleep with pleasure (closing her eyes and thinking "господи, какой же кайф просто вот так лежать с закрытыми глазами" — God, what bliss just to lie like this with closed eyes) and wakes with pleasure, anticipating the day. The contrast is the mode "как бы дотянуть до вечера / выходных / отпуска" (how to hold on until evening / the weekend / vacation). She admits that sometimes from the very morning she feels she needs rest — "what can you expect from such a day?" [msg #280]
3. **Desire to live and attention to small things.** When she feels the desire to live, does every simplest action with attention and pleasure, notices reflections of light on walls and birdsong, and has time and wish simply to look out the window. [msg #280]
4. **Energy not spilled.** When she did not spill energy on fuss, checking social media, squabbles and useless conflicts, and did not dry herself out with meetings and calls ("колами"). [msg #280]
5. **Breathers.** When she gave herself pauses instead of jumping "как угорелая" (like a madwoman) between tasks, apps and browser tabs. [msg #280]
6. **One thing at a time.** When she did one thing at a time and was not distracted by thoughts of what else needs doing while doing what is in front of her. [msg #280]
7. **Being unavailable.** When she is unavailable and herself chooses the time and place for interactions with, and influences from, the outside world. [msg #280]
8. **No inner self-eating.** When she did not "eat herself" inside, noticed and responded to internal conflicts and confusion, and practised mindfulness and self-observation. [msg #280]
9. **Care and planned rest.** When she treated her body, energy and resource with care, and understood that rest is a process and an activity aimed at restoring them, which happens only when competently planned — emphasised in the essay as "Когда. Грамотно. Планировала." (When. I. Competently. Planned. [rest]). [msg #280]
10. **Attending to state — and acting on it.** Not only constantly asking herself "Как я сейчас?" (How am I now?), but listening to and hearing the answer, reacting to what she heard, and having a repertoire of actions for doing so. The lesson: check-ins without a response repertoire are not enough. [msg #280]
11. **Interest and compassion.** When she treated herself and life with interest and compassion, and valued life. [msg #280]

Closing: "Отдохнувшая ли я? Пока не до конца, но я туда иду." (Am I rested? Not fully yet, but I'm heading there.) Context: Katya named 2025 her "годом нетоксичного развития" (year of non-toxic development), which she says is turning out to be more a "year of getting rid of illusions". It has two foci — development (how to grow her practice and income more systematically; for this she works with a coach, plans more clearly and keeps reports) and recovery (because in the growth process it is easy to spill what is always in short supply; for this she found like-minded women and created the closed club «Отдохнувшие» for those for whom it matters to keep focus on rest). [msg #280]

### Observation, people, regularity — three supports for keeping pace
Katya's origin story: a year before November 2025 she realised she wanted to help people rest, but did NOT launch an open group, because she had no strength herself ("надо самой отдохнуть, Катя" — you need to rest yourself, Katya) and did not feel like an expert in rest — "Скорее, двоечница" (more of a failing student). It seemed she first had to learn to be rested herself. She then recalled what helps her keep pace in other spheres: "Наблюдение. Люди. Регулярность." (Observation. People. Regularity.) [msg #450]

1. **Observation** — systematically recording one's energy (implemented as the weekly check-in diary below). [msg #450]
2. **People** — a closed group "только для своих" (only for one's own people): like-minded women ready to devote a year to researching energy and resource. There turned out to be seven: Katya, Olya, Anya, Natasha, Radmila, Yana and Karina. [msg #450]
3. **Regularity** — fixed rhythm: monthly meetings plus weekly check-ins, which created social commitment. [msg #450]

### Weekly energy check-in diary plus monthly group meeting
How the closed group operated, per Katya: [msg #450]
1. **Set rest goals for the year** together at the start. [msg #450]
2. **Meet once a month** to discuss what worked and what did not, and to plan the next month. [msg #450]
3. **Weekly check-in.** Katya promised the group a weekly check-in; once promised, it felt "неприлично сливаться" (indecent to bail), so the diary "stuck" — for the first time she managed to keep a diary for 10.5 months. [msg #450]
4. **Why it works:** if you record your energy every week, you begin to notice. When week after week you write down the same thing that wears you out, you can no longer "развидеть" (unsee) it — you have to change it. At some point the other members also felt the effect and started checking in. [msg #450]
5. **Bring something new to report.** It is pleasant to write something new in the check-in and monthly plan, so Katya regularly tried new things — this fed the experimental approach. [msg #450]

### Experimental approach — hypothesis → test → change
"Цели ничто. Эксперименты все." (Goals are nothing. Experiments are everything.) Katya found fairly quickly that goals are not the best choice for rest, especially when one's level of working on it is still at zero: life inevitably happens, habits take over, and roughly everything goes wrong. So she switched on her favourite approach (and, she says, her clients' favourite) — the experimental one: "Гопотеза → проверка → изменение." [sic] (Hypothesis → test → change.) [msg #450]
1. **Hypothesis** — pick something to try for rest/energy. [msg #450]
2. **Test** — try it during the month and record in check-ins. [msg #450]
3. **Change** — keep, adjust or drop, based on what was observed, and plan the next experiment at the monthly meeting. [msg #450]

### Laboratory outcomes — "Что изменится?" (What will change?)
The open laboratory card lists what a participant is meant to build and experience over six weeks. [img #495]
1. **Reframe rest** — understand that rest is not a luxury or reward but a skill that can be developed and built into life. [img #495]
2. **List of fatigue signs** — compile your own list of signs of tiredness so you can stop before "полного обесточивания" (total power-out). [img #495]
3. **Rest formula** — assemble your own "формулу отдыха": how to support yourself on a regular basis. [img #495]
4. **Express first-aid kit** — "экспресс-аптечку на случай «пожара»": concrete ways to recover when there is no strength at all. [img #495]
5. **Early signals** — start noticing the first signals of fatigue and manage to stop not at the moment when it is already "всё" (all over) but a bit earlier. [img #495]
Effects promised: (01) mood improves, inspiration grows, clarity and lightness are felt; (02) energy appears — not the explosive kind from coffee, sugar and cheap dopamine, but calm and stable; (03) a sense of inner support forms: you know when to stop and how to support yourself. [img #495]

The lab is framed as "6 недель наблюдений, открытий и мягких перемен" (6 weeks of observations, discoveries and gentle changes) for those who want to stop coping, proving and fighting, and instead learn to rest and start living. Format described: 6 weeks, 5 meetings, a group chat with Katya's feedback, more than 10 practices, and theory based on research and her own experience; participants leave with their own recovery formula and "аптечку" (first-aid kit), and materials stay with them. After the active six weeks she proposed a slow format of one meeting a month for the rest of the year if enough people wanted it. There was also a tariff with deeper individual work, positioned as a test-drive of one-to-one coaching. [img #465, msg #465, #491]

### Client rest-coaching protocol — the Anya case
Purpose: Katya's description (shared with the client's consent) of 4.5 months of one-to-one work with Anya, whose goal was "Впервые в жизни хочу научиться отдыхать" (For the first time in my life I want to learn to rest). [msg #598]

**Starting point.** Anya: entrepreneur, fourth year in emigration, business in Moscow, works from home with no boundary between work and life; believes she must be reachable for Moscow at 7 a.m. London time; endless phone and work chats; notices fatigue only when already lying down (previously this happened with pneumonia, now "just like that"). Her beliefs: "Надо преодолевать" (You have to overcome), "Отдых — только когда сделала все дела" (Rest only when all tasks are done), "Много отдыхать стыдно" (Resting a lot is shameful), "Чем больше усилий, тем больше достигаешь" (The more effort, the more you achieve), "Круто надо делать всегда" (You must always do things brilliantly) — plus an image of the entrepreneur broadcast by media and family: red-eyed, always at maximum. [msg #598]

Steps (as listed by Katya):
1. **Start with beliefs.** Name them and replace them — "Пока их не назовешь и не заменишь, они продолжают управлять" (Until you name and replace them, they keep running things). [msg #598]
2. **Collect fatigue signs.** Anya's: sleeping badly, skipping sport, body aches, a feeling that something is stuck inside. Fatigue has concrete symptoms that can be learned to read earlier than the body puts you down with pneumonia. [msg #598]
3. **Build safety cushions** — the minimum that holds even in hard periods, "не роскошь, а база" (not a luxury but the base). Anya's: reading before sleep, 9+ hours of sleep, walks with the dog. [msg #598]
4. **Write principles of the rested state** — specifically Anya's version, not universal internet advice but what works for her: work 5–6 hours split into before lunch and after; lunch with a walk; sleep as much as she wants; pleasure — every day something from "хочу" (want) rather than "надо" (must): a book, a walk for herself, a relaxed film, actions out of curiosity. [msg #598]
5. **Work with subpersonalities.** They found "Аня на максималках" (Anya at max) — always fully battle-ready, cannot stop, thinks "what will others say?", turns even rest into something correct, intensive and over-stuffed — and "Аню отдохнувшую" (rested Anya) — engaged, says "no" when something does not match her interests, allows herself simply to go out with the dog. The aim was NOT to kill one and grow the other: "Anya at max" helped build the business and cope with emigration. They learned to notice when she switches on too intensely and out of place. [msg #598]
6. **Count real working hours, then ask how many she wants.** It turned out Anya's success and the business's success are not measured in hours at all — "a simple thought that is hard to accept when the whole coordinate system is built around the amount of effort". [msg #598]
7. **Other work** — Katya adds "И много чего еще…" (and much more). (!) INCOMPLETE: the remaining steps are not described. [msg #598]

**Results.** Anya works 5–6 hours a day and does more than she used to in a strained 10; sets clear hours for contact with Moscow; notices fatigue before she "crashes"; knows her safety cushions and does not bargain with them even in busy periods; is creating her own image of an entrepreneur; says "no" when something does not match her interests; switched from phone to a book in the evening; started sleeping in a separate bedroom (sleep improved for both her and her husband); took her first vacation without work; allowed herself rest without a plan or usefulness. Katya's reading: the main result is a new contact with oneself; habits appear by themselves after it. [msg #598]

### Personal rest research plan (Anya's goal structure)
An image in the Anya post shows how her goal was formulated as a research project: [img #598]
- Goal: conduct a study of rest and, for the first time in life, learn to rest — in order to feel inspired, filled and happy more often and for longer.
- Research: (1) figure out what rest is for me — how, when and how much I need to rest; (2) understand which beliefs, habits and convictions hinder and which help; (3) see what fills/rests me and what takes energy; (4) find new ways of support and resource; (5) determine which activities can absorb me as much as work does; (6) "и др." (an open-ended continuation in the original).

### Seven shifts from health coaching — the Sasha case
A client's forwarded post about six months with Katya. She came with a request about food and habits: through 2025 she had very little energy, much anxiety, weight was rising and health was sending signals. She thought it was a task with a technical solution; it was not. [msg #628, img #628]
1. **Changed the starting point.** For several months they discussed that without rest there is no energy and without energy there are no changes; she had been trying to start from a very low base. As soon as she really rested, the process went: energy appeared and weight began to fall with the same efforts. [msg #628]
2. **Started noticing what she already does.** Walking, cycling, dog walks, yoga "не считалось" (didn't count) because it was not the gym or weekly hikes. That bar never lowers however much she does, and it is precisely this bar that eats her energy faster than any load. She began to notice "small steps". [msg #628]
3. **Stopped motivating herself with anxiety.** Anxiety as a "морковка сзади" (carrot from behind) moves you but burns you. She began to watch this and sees she has become steadier; her Oura ring helps here. [msg #628]
4. **Saw roles beyond work.** For a couple of years she lived only for work; now she notices other roles — mother, wife, friend, student. She enrolled in the second level of Gestalt training, did a German bootcamp, returned to mentoring: she wanted to live and to have something besides work and the anxiety around it. [msg #628]
5. **Started living during business trips** so that not all routines collapse: watching what she eats, trying to get enough sleep; soon she expects to pack trainers again. [msg #628]
6. **Allowed herself to accept help.** Trainer, doctor, coach, psychotherapist — helping specialists strengthen her. She "hacked" German medicine: did all the check-ups she had postponed, changed her main doctor, got proper recommendations and referrals. At physical training she set boundaries with the supervisor at once, so that they guide and do not shame; she trains with a friend, which supports her. [msg #628]
7. **Noticing self-care again.** Massage, a walk without headphones, vitamins, sleeping on time are not trifles. Knowing she tends to see only what is unfinished, it is worth deliberately noticing what works. Being able to talk about self-care turned out to be linked to being able to talk about her successes in general — which she does badly, especially at work. [msg #628]

Her main conclusions: work had to be done not with the symptom but with the state it grows from; follow desire rather than invent what is "правильно" (correct); if you know you tend to scold yourself, praise yourself deliberately — "Это не самолюбование, это гигиена" (This is not vanity, it is hygiene). "Я шла разбираться с едой. А разобралась с тем, как я с собой живу." (I went to sort out food and sorted out how I live with myself.) Her next steps: (1) add one fitness activity a week; (2) think about movement on business trips and see what works; (3) notice more often the good that already exists and find a format for it besides regular massage. She adds that with Katya she manages to look "вне рамок" (outside the frames). [msg #628]

## Detailed insights

### What the closed group gave Katya (10 months)
- **Acceptance of slumps through seeing others' cycles.** "Если будешь слишком быстро гнать — слишком быстро приедешь." (If you drive too fast you'll arrive too fast.) Slumps are, first, unavoidable — and very cruel if you squeeze yourself — and second, they happen to absolutely everyone. A favourite group ritual: laughing at one's illusion "ну я-то особенная и всемогущая" (well, I'm special and all-powerful), then jointly admitting: no, not special (maybe just a little). [msg #450]
- **Permission to be without energy.** Especially when work requires shining with energy in sessions and blogs, one sometimes wants to whine, be vulnerable, without inspiration or strength, and still feel loved, supported and considered the best. It is priceless to hear "Катя, может, тебе нужен отпуск?" (Katya, maybe you need a vacation?) and reluctantly agree — because she had long thought so herself but did not want to notice it and allow it. [msg #450]
- **Mirroring.** Katya "will never stop singing odes" to group work, calling it a treasure trove of insights: you listen to someone else and suddenly realise "у меня же точно также" (it's exactly the same with me); it is as if someone highlighted your shadow; you can no longer unsee the problem and cannot not start solving it. [msg #450]
- **Community.** In emigration Katya lacked community and like-minded people; the group became an outlet, and the women became friends who now discuss life plans, money and family as well as energy. [msg #450]
- **Theory alongside practice.** Besides practical insights she studied research and articles and had read 4 books on rest, and does not plan to stop. (!) INCOMPLETE: the books are not named in these messages. [msg #450]

### What members say the group gave them
- **Radmila Khakova** (writer, blogger, podcast host): the group gives support of like-minded women and returns focus to recovery; it is "легальное время на рефлексию" (legal time for reflection) and a place to speak openly about difficulties and ask for support. Most important is exchange of experience — what worked, what didn't, what works for others; the women sometimes generate ideas specifically for her, creating fertile ground for a new relationship with rest. She saw that people can get tired in any circumstances, and that rest does not arise by itself — it must be fitted to real life; "Это навык" (It's a skill). For her it is about small steps suited to her situation and about building recovery when time is fragmented ("время рваное"). The group showed that everyone is alive with different lives and that this is normal, and became about life, support, resilience and ways of being gentler to oneself. [img #466, #493]
- **Olya Bastron** (ICF coach): the group gave colossal support in her year of moving, when she lost her familiar close circle; it was a space to bring both whining and joy. At the start of the year she did not want to admit her energy level had changed. Her journey had two phases: first she explored where energy leaks and tried various techniques; in the second came acceptance — she saw cyclicity, understood that the base — "сон, стресс, отдых, питание, природа" (sleep, stress, rest, nutrition, nature) — really works, and rebuilt her life around the amount of strength she has now. She accepted she is "не special" and cannot ignore the body's rhythms and needs; became more tolerant and gentle with herself; rest turned from rare snatches into a daily regime. Without the group she would have kept grinding "по старой карте развития" (by the old map of development) and been harsher to herself; in the group one can be "не такой" (not right) this week, out of resource, and not count it as failure. Her inner voice now sounds softer. [img #467, #492]
- **Karina Lvova** (entrepreneur, business consultant): the group helped her move from theory to regular practice; when rest and fatigue regularly enter one's field of attention, there is less chance of drifting far down the familiar crooked path. Examples of groupmates — real women with children, jobs, piles of work — motivated and supported her. She began to plan rest both before and after loads, strengthen routines in busy and stressful periods, and set up actions for regular rest during the week. She used to think rest was a luxury; after observing, she found it is a necessity for her to live and work well. Unexpectedly the group about rest became a group about life: a space of support, of timely questions she would not have asked herself, and of frank conversations with real experience. She loves the video circles members send each other — from floating, sound healing, from rest under a palm tree — which motivate her to stop and schedule rest for herself. [img #468, #494]

### Who the laboratory is aimed at — signs you need to learn to rest
