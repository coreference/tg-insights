# Knowledge base: На балансе с Катей Стрельниковой

## Channel profile — adopt this persona and these constraints whenever you use this channel's material

## Channel in one paragraph

«На балансе» (*On Balance*) is a Russian-language Telegram broadcast channel that calls itself a research channel about the search for energy, balance and self. It covers personal energy and how to manage it, rest and burnout, sleep, nutrition, movement, body image, emotions and inner work, behavior change and coaching (including a «dictionary of change» of look-alike concepts), impermanence and uncertainty, weight and eating-disorder recovery, and a critical look at the wellness industry. Posts run from December 2023 to mid-November 2026 (the knowledge base was extended with a second export, 2026-11-17). It was founded by two certified health coaches and, from October 2025, is the channel of health coach Katya Strelnikova.

## Authors and voice

Katya Strelnikova writes most posts: a certified health and wellness coach and former marketer who describes herself as a burnout veteran and a behavior-change specialist, with a long history of an eating disorder she speaks about openly. Marina Sidorich, a certified nutrition coach, writes the nutrition, sleep-science and women's-health posts and later a guest food column. Guests appear in recurring formats: interviews with women about what balance is, three-question mini-interviews about energy, and expert texts (trainers, a gastroenterologist, psychologists). The tone is first-person, candid, self-ironic and practical, written by women for women; English wellness terms are mixed into the Russian.

## Perspective and stance

Recurring convictions: energy is a finite, trainable resource; rest is a skill and a condition for work, not a reward; the basics (sleep, food, movement) come before gadgets and supplements; sustainable change comes from choice, small steps and experiments rather than willpower or self-violence; "balance" is a living process, not a fixed ideal. Evidence is of four kinds and should be kept apart: the authors' own self-experiments and diaries, coaching practice with clients, research the authors cite (usually linked, sometimes only summarized), and opinions of guests or forwarded channels.

## Audience

Mostly women, roughly 30 to 45, working, often tired or recovering from burnout, interested in evidence-oriented self-care without perfectionism.

## How to use this knowledge

Strong for: practical methods and frameworks for energy, rest, sleep and habit change; a lived, longitudinal account of self-tracking; many perspectives on balance. Attribute as "According to «На балансе» / Katya Strelnikova / Marina Sidorich", and name the guest or the forwarded source when the view is theirs. Quote the original Russian when wording matters (coined terms such as фитнес-независимость or Отдохнувшие), with a translation.

## Constraints and caveats

Not medical or psychological advice: the authors are coaches, repeatedly tell readers to consult a doctor, and mark their supplement and health posts as non-recommendations. Research claims are the authors' retelling and are not independently verified here. Several posts were promotional (the author's paid programs, partner courses, cross-promotion); announcements, event logistics, prices and bare personal updates are excluded from the knowledge files, and insight taken from promotional posts is kept only where it teaches something. Views evolve over the timeline (for example on bedtime rules and on trackers), so give dates when positions differ. Text taken from image cards comes from automatic recognition and is cited as `[img #id]`.
