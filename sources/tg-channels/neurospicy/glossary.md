# Glossary — Нейропряность

Recurring terms, jargon and coined concepts with source-grounded definitions.

- **"рецепты" спокойствия** — personal "recipes" of calm and comfort; the workbook's intended output [msg #532] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **"светофор"** — traffic light — energy-accounting method: each activity coloured red (drains), green (restores), yellow (everything else) [msg #132] · [Pacing and energy accounting](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/pacing-energy-accounting.md)
- **"чековая книжка" энергии** — energy "checkbook" — energy-accounting method named in the pacing article [msg #132] · [Pacing and energy accounting](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/pacing-energy-accounting.md)
- **8D аудио** — 8D audio: sound processed with equalisation, panning and effects so it seems to move around the listener's head [msg #351] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **ABA** — behavioural therapy, widespread in the USA as a "treatment" for autism, cited as a formal way autistic people are trained to mask [msg #24] · [Masking, camouflaging and unmasking](topics/masking-identity/masking-unmasking.md)
- **ABSC (Чеклист симптомов аутистического выгорания)** — Autistic Burnout Symptom Checklist, a non-standardised self-tracking tool from Embrace Autism [msg #31, #386] · [Autistic burnout: definition, causes and warning signs](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/autistic-burnout-signs.md)
- **AuDHDer** — an autistic person with ADHD [msg #41] · [Sensory processing, overload and sensory regulation](topics/nervous-system-senses/sensory-processing-overload.md)
- **BVD** — binocular vision dysfunction: the eyes do not work together as one system [msg #349] · [The neurodivergent comorbidity cluster and self-help map](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/comorbidity-cluster.md)
- **CAT-Q** — camouflaging questionnaire whose high scores were associated with thwarted belongingness (Cassidy et al., 2020) [msg #24] · [Masking, camouflaging and unmasking](topics/masking-identity/masking-unmasking.md)
- **Cntnap2** — gene mutation used as the mouse model in the Z944 study, ~1% of human ASD cases [msg #718] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **de-novo мутации** — mutations that arise for the first time in the person and are absent in the parents [msg #658] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **deep pressure (глубокое давление)** — strong or deep pressure — can be a trigger for some and a calming stimulus (hugs, weighted blanket) for others [msg #167] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **DMN (сеть работы мозга по умолчанию)** — default mode network [msg #130, #317] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **EFT («Постукивание эмоциональной свободы»)** — Emotional Freedom Techniques (tapping), named by the channel as a way to cope with anxiety [msg #533] · [Anxiety and panic: CBT and body-based coping](topics/trauma-anxiety/anxiety-panic-coping.md)
- **LDN (Low dose Naltrexone)** — low-dose naltrexone, discussed for fibromyalgia and ME/CFS [msg #826] · [POTS, dysautonomia, ME/CFS and post-COVID](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/pots-mecfs-postcovid.md)
- **LDN (низкие дозы налтрексона)** — low-dose naltrexone, used to stabilise mast cells [msg #690, #826, #827] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **MTHFR** — genetic mutation impairing folate metabolism, linked to high homocysteine and poor breakdown of catecholamines [msg #34, #36] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md); [The neurodivergent comorbidity cluster and self-help map](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/comorbidity-cluster.md)
- **P-TPO** — thyroid peroxidase antibodies, the lab marker the author tracked [msg #827] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **PNI-52** — Pathological Narcissism Inventory, tool separating grandiose and vulnerable narcissism [msg #725] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **Road to 2026** — international project of The Ehlers-Danlos Society to revise hEDS/HSD criteria [msg #603] · [Ehlers-Danlos syndrome and hypermobility](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/ehlers-danlos-hypermobility.md)
- **salience network (SN)** — network that detects and filters environmental stimuli, separates the important from the secondary and steers attention accordingly [msg #130] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **U-MAP** — Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection, a machine-learning method used to cluster symptoms [msg #663] · [Differential diagnosis: autism, ADHD and look-alike conditions](topics/diagnosis-research/differential-diagnosis.md)
- **Z944** — T-type calcium channel blocker originally developed for epilepsy, tested in Cntnap2 mice [msg #718] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **«без ожиданий»** — "no expectations" — a calendar label for time set aside purely to recover [msg #315] · [Accommodations, self-checks and daily supports](topics/daily-life-communication/accommodations-daily-supports.md)
- **«бой или полет»** — "fight or flight" (literal Russian rendering of fight-or-flight): the automatic survival response that prepares the body to fight or flee [msg #295] · [Anxiety and panic: CBT and body-based coping](topics/trauma-anxiety/anxiety-panic-coping.md)
- **«Не позволяй аутизму определять тебя»** — "Don't let autism define you" — a well-meant phrase that autistic authors criticise as implying autism is bad or shameful [msg #334] · [Autistic identity, community and disability pride](topics/masking-identity/autistic-identity-community.md)
- **«отключка»** — "shutdown"; a state of switching off that high-maskers push through and that should be pre-empted by rest [msg #613] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **АВМ** — arteriovenous malformation - an MRI finding later revised to a harmless venous angioma [msg #256] · [Medical gaslighting and medical trauma](topics/trauma-anxiety/medical-gaslighting-trauma.md)
- **адаптивное отстранение** — adaptive withdrawal — social withdrawal used as a strategy to manage limited energy (burnout) [msg #589] · [Autistic burnout versus depression](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-vs-depression.md)
- **АИТ (аутоиммунный тиреоидит)** — autoimmune thyroiditis [msg #827] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **аккомодации** — accommodations — adjustments at work or school (flexible hours, quiet space, sensory-friendly setting, communication accommodations) [msg #167, #312, #575] · [Recognizing autism in adults: criteria, hidden traits and late diagnosis](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-recognition-adults.md); [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **аккомодации и адаптации** — accommodations and adaptations — the changes a particular person needs in order to function, as much a part of their uniqueness as their strengths [msg #266, #315] · [Accommodations, self-checks and daily supports](topics/daily-life-communication/accommodations-daily-supports.md)
- **алекситимия** — alexithymia — difficulty recognizing, noticing, understanding and describing one's own sensations and emotions; not a diagnosis [msg #47, #49, #305, #605, #799] · [Complex trauma (C-PTSD) in neurodivergent lives](topics/trauma-anxiety/complex-trauma-neurodivergent.md); [Interoception, alexithymia and naming emotions](topics/nervous-system-senses/interoception-alexithymia.md)
- **аллистический** — allistic; non-autistic (the article's gloss: "аллистических (не аутичных)") [msg #40] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **аллистичные люди** — allistic people — non-autistic people [msg #534] · [Meltdowns and shutdowns](topics/nervous-system-senses/meltdowns-shutdowns.md)
- **аллистичный (allistic)** — non-autistic [msg #496] · [Masking, camouflaging and unmasking](topics/masking-identity/masking-unmasking.md)
- **анализы «в норме»** — tests "within normal range" — the typical situation in which complex patients are not believed [msg #536] · [Talking to doctors and patient self-advocacy](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/patient-self-advocacy.md)
- **ангедония** — anhedonia — loss of ability to enjoy previously pleasant activities (depression side) [msg #193] · [Autistic burnout versus depression](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-vs-depression.md)
- **анмаскинг** — unmasking: exploring one's authenticity rather than imitating others (including imitating other autistic people) [msg #398, #560, #575] · [Autistic burnout: definition, causes and warning signs](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/autistic-burnout-signs.md); [Autistic identity, community and disability pride](topics/masking-identity/autistic-identity-community.md); [Recognizing autism in adults: criteria, hidden traits and late diagnosis](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-recognition-adults.md)
- **анмаскинг (unmasking)** — dropping masking; reframed in the channel as excavation/emergence of a self rather than removal of a mask [msg #73, #660] · [Masking, camouflaging and unmasking](topics/masking-identity/masking-unmasking.md)
- **Аспергеры (Аспи)** — "Aspergers / Aspie"; a term largely rejected by the community [msg #558] · [Autistic identity, community and disability pride](topics/masking-identity/autistic-identity-community.md)
- **ассимиляция (assimilation)** — strategies used to try to fit in to social situations [msg #156] · [Masking, camouflaging and unmasking](topics/masking-identity/masking-unmasking.md)
- **аура / предприступное ощущение** — aura / pre-seizure sensation - the warning feeling before an attack [msg #256] · [Medical gaslighting and medical trauma](topics/trauma-anxiety/medical-gaslighting-trauma.md)
- **Аутистическая инерция** — autistic inertia: comfort in staying in the current state, distinguished from PDA where the person wants to change but cannot overcome the demand [msg #29] · [Recognizing autism in adults: criteria, hidden traits and late diagnosis](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-recognition-adults.md)
- **аутистическое / аутичное выгорание** — autistic burnout: pervasive, long-term exhaustion, loss of function and reduced tolerance to stimuli caused by chronic life stress and a mismatch of expectations and abilities without adequate support [msg #28, #392] · [Autistic burnout: definition, causes and warning signs](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/autistic-burnout-signs.md)
- **аутистическое выгорание** — autistic burnout; a state of physical, emotional and mental exhaustion marked by chronic exhaustion, reduced tolerance to stimuli and loss of skills [msg #40] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **аутистическое выгорание (autistic burnout)** — burnout to which the cost of masking leads [msg #24, #156] · [Masking, camouflaging and unmasking](topics/masking-identity/masking-unmasking.md)
- **аутичное / аутистическое выгорание** — autistic burnout — community-coined term for extreme exhaustion after prolonged or acute stress; per Raymaker et al. a syndrome of pervasive long-term exhaustion, loss of function and reduced tolerance to stimuli caused by chronic life stress and a mismatch of expectations and abilities without adequate support [msg #28, #193] · [Autistic burnout versus depression](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-vs-depression.md)
- **аутичное выгорание** — autistic burnout — a long-lasting state of global exhaustion, loss of skills and heightened sensitivity to the environment [msg #167, #434] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **аутичный / Autistic** — identity-first term preferred by most of the community over "person with autism" [msg #559] · [Autistic identity, community and disability pride](topics/masking-identity/autistic-identity-community.md)
- **Аффективная эмпатия** — affective (emotional) empathy: emotional contagion; feeling another's emotions without always being able to name them [msg #285] · [Autistic social communication and processing](topics/daily-life-communication/autistic-social-communication.md)
- **БА (болезнь Альцгеймера)** — Alzheimer's disease [msg #318] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **бадди / body double** — body doubling: two people working side by side at the same time, each on their own task, to overcome executive-dysfunction paralysis [msg #431, #830] · [ADHD traits: rejection sensitivity, daydreaming and body doubling](topics/daily-life-communication/adhd-traits-tools.md)
- **БАР** — bipolar affective disorder (bipolar disorder); БАР-I = bipolar I [msg #598, #663] · [Differential diagnosis: autism, ADHD and look-alike conditions](topics/diagnosis-research/differential-diagnosis.md)
- **БДР (большое депрессивное расстройство)** — major depressive disorder, MDD [msg #192] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **безопасная еда (safe food)** — safe food — predictable food that brings comfort, lowers anxiety and fits sensory preferences [msg #167] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **безопасная еда / «та же самая еда»** — safe food / "same food" — food that brings comfort, causes no sensory problems and to which the person is attached [msg #701] · [Accommodations, self-checks and daily supports](topics/daily-life-communication/accommodations-daily-supports.md)
- **Билатеральная стимуляция (БЛС)** — bilateral stimulation: alternating stimulation of the left and right hemispheres, visual, auditory or tactile; key part of EMDR [msg #351] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **биомаркеры** — biomarkers: measurable biological signs that could reveal biological causes of mental disorders instead of symptom-based diagnosis [msg #192] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **Блуждающий нерв** — vagus nerve: tenth cranial nerve, largest parasympathetic nerve, controlling heart rate, breathing, blood pressure, digestion [msg #35, #159] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **борьба с беспокойными мыслями** — challenging anxious thoughts: identifying panic thoughts and evaluating whether they are realistic [msg #295] · [Anxiety and panic: CBT and body-based coping](topics/trauma-anxiety/anxiety-panic-coping.md)
- **Буквальное восприятие** — literal perception: taking questions, criteria and questionnaires literally [msg #401, #805] · [Recognizing autism in adults: criteria, hidden traits and late diagnosis](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-recognition-adults.md)
- **валидность** — validity: diagnostic categories reflect the true nature of mental disorders [msg #192] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **Вентральная вагальная система** — ventral vagal system: front branch of the vagus that lets sympathetic and parasympathetic systems work in harmony [msg #35] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **Вербальный шатдаун** — verbal shutdown — temporary loss of speech as a reaction to stress or overload; speech returns when the nervous system settles [msg #736] · [Differential diagnosis: autism, ADHD and look-alike conditions](topics/diagnosis-research/differential-diagnosis.md); [Meltdowns and shutdowns](topics/nervous-system-senses/meltdowns-shutdowns.md)
- **вестибулярная система** — vestibular system; inner-ear sense of position, balance and movement in space [msg #25] · [Sensory processing, overload and sensory regulation](topics/nervous-system-senses/sensory-processing-overload.md)
- **визуальный снег** — visual snow; a constant visual symptom of seeing millions of flickering particles, like TV static [msg #137] · [Sensory processing, overload and sensory regulation](topics/nervous-system-senses/sensory-processing-overload.md)
- **внутренний / интернализированный эйблизм** — internalized ableism: ableism directed at oneself (or held by one's family), destroying mental health and sense of identity [msg #448, #615] · [Internalized capitalism, ableism and self-worth](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/internalized-capitalism-ableism.md)
- **внутренний критик** — inner critic: the self-critical inner voice that intensifies in autistic burnout [msg #23, #33] · [Internalized capitalism, ableism and self-worth](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/internalized-capitalism-ableism.md)
- **внутренний мелтдаун** — internal meltdown — a meltdown that is felt with full intensity inside but looks like nothing is happening from outside [msg #367] · [Meltdowns and shutdowns](topics/nervous-system-senses/meltdowns-shutdowns.md)
- **внутренний эйблизм (эйбелизм)** — internalized ableism; beliefs and biases taken on from living in an ableist society that shape unrealistic expectations of oneself [msg #40] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **Возбуждение / Валентность** — arousal (energy, autonomic activation) and valence (pleasantness) — the two axes of the emotion matrix [msg #38] · [Interoception, alexithymia and naming emotions](topics/nervous-system-senses/interoception-alexithymia.md)
- **восстанавливающие занятия** — restorative activities; activities that give sensory pleasure, comfort and calm [msg #532] · [Recovering from neurodivergent burnout](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-recovery.md)
- **ВСД** — VSD, vegetative-vascular dystonia - a post-Soviet catch-all label the author mocks as the punchline of her discharge papers [msg #256] · [Medical gaslighting and medical trauma](topics/trauma-anxiety/medical-gaslighting-trauma.md)
- **ВСР (вариабельность сердечного ритма)** — HRV, heart rate variability: how much the heart rate varies over time and situations; indicator of vagal tone and of the state of the nervous system [msg #35, #159, #500] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **второе мнение** — second opinion from another specialist [msg #539] · [Talking to doctors and patient self-advocacy](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/patient-self-advocacy.md)
- **Вывих** — dislocation: the bones of a joint fully separate [msg #125] · [Ehlers-Danlos syndrome and hypermobility](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/ehlers-danlos-hypermobility.md)
- **высокофункциональность** — "high-functioning": per Mackereth, a label measuring how useful a person is to others, not how they feel or cope inside [msg #687] · [Internalized capitalism, ableism and self-worth](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/internalized-capitalism-ableism.md)
- **газлайтинг** — gaslighting; covert manipulation that makes a person doubt their reality and sanity [msg #28, #785] · [Autistic burnout versus depression](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-vs-depression.md); [Complex trauma (C-PTSD) in neurodivergent lives](topics/trauma-anxiety/complex-trauma-neurodivergent.md)
- **ГГН ось** — hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis; the basis of the body's stress-response system [msg #217] · [Complex trauma (C-PTSD) in neurodivergent lives](topics/trauma-anxiety/complex-trauma-neurodivergent.md)
- **ГГН-ось** — HPA axis (hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal), altered by early trauma, PTSD or chronic stress [msg #689] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **гексагон аутичных потребностей (6C)** — hexagon of autistic needs — six needs whose Russian names all start with С [msg #772] · [Autistic identity, community and disability pride](topics/masking-identity/autistic-identity-community.md)
- **гематоэнцефалический барьер** — blood–brain barrier, whose integrity mast cell mediators can disrupt [msg #318] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **Гиперактивация** — hyperarousal: state outside the window dominated by the sympathetic system (anxiety, aggression, fear, fast heart rate) [msg #35, #103, #159] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **гиперактивация / гипоактивация** — hyperarousal (fight/flight) / hypoarousal (freeze) states in which the nervous system gets stuck [msg #388, #446] · [Autistic burnout: definition, causes and warning signs](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/autistic-burnout-signs.md)
- **гипервигилантность / гипер-внимательность** — hypervigilance; constant alertness, even in safe places [msg #301, #648] · [Complex trauma (C-PTSD) in neurodivergent lives](topics/trauma-anxiety/complex-trauma-neurodivergent.md)
- **гиперкомпенсация** — overcompensation — adapting to expectations by over-exerting, alongside masking [msg #790] · [Autistic burnout versus depression](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/burnout-vs-depression.md)
- **гипернезависимость** — hyper-independence: listed as a sign of internalized ableism [msg #615] · [Internalized capitalism, ableism and self-worth](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/internalized-capitalism-ableism.md)
- **гиперфантазия** — hyperphantasia: the ability to create extremely detailed and vivid mental images [msg #155] · [ADHD traits: rejection sensitivity, daydreaming and body doubling](topics/daily-life-communication/adhd-traits-tools.md)
- **Гипоактивация** — hypoarousal: state outside the window dominated by the parasympathetic/dorsal vagal system (fatigue, apathy, detachment); also another name for dorsal vagal shutdown [msg #35, #60, #159] · [Regulating the neurodivergent nervous system](topics/nervous-system-senses/nervous-system-regulation.md)
- **гипоконнективность / гиперконнективность** — hypo-/hyperconnectivity of the insula seen in different autistic people [msg #186] · [Interoception, alexithymia and naming emotions](topics/nervous-system-senses/interoception-alexithymia.md)
- **гистамин** — histamine: immune-system chemical produced in response to allergens, injury or stress; also a brain neurotransmitter [msg #91, #93] · [Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS)](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/mast-cell-activation.md)
- **гнездование** — "nesting": needing time to withdraw at home, an early stress sign on the colour scale [msg #372] · [Autistic burnout: definition, causes and warning signs](topics/neurodivergent-burnout/autistic-burnout-signs.md)
- **гомеостаз** — homeostasis — a system's striving to stay in equilibrium, maintained by feedback loops like a thermostat [msg #37, #186] · [Interoception, alexithymia and naming emotions](topics/nervous-system-senses/interoception-alexithymia.md)

- **грандиозный нарциссизм** — grandiose narcissism: exploitation, self-aggrandizement [msg #725] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)

- **гСЭД (ДСТ), СПОТ, САТК** — hEDS (connective tissue disorder), POTS, MCAS [msg #718, #755] · [Reading autism and ADHD research critically](topics/diagnosis-research/autism-research-critique.md)
- **гСЭД / hEDS** — hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, a hereditary connective-tissue disorder [msg #509, #779] · [The neurodivergent comorbidity cluster and self-help map](topics/comorbid-chronic-conditions/comorbidity-cluster.md)
