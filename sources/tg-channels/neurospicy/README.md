## About the channel

## Channel in one paragraph

«Нейропряность» ("Neurospicy") is a Russian-language Telegram channel, February 2024 to October 2026, that translates English-language material on autism, ADHD and their comorbidities for the Russian-speaking community. Its subjects are autistic and neurodivergent burnout, masking, the neurodiversity paradigm, recognizing autism and ADHD in adults, nervous-system and sensory regulation, interoception, meltdowns, trauma and C-PTSD, medical gaslighting, and comorbid chronic conditions (hEDS/HSD, MCAS, POTS, ME/CFS). It also runs a Discord server for peer support.

## Authors and voice

The author is Настя (Nastya), who introduced herself at 32. She was diagnosed with ADHD and severe depression at almost 29 and with autism (РАС) six months later. She identifies as AuDHD. She describes herself mainly as a translator of long articles. Most posts are translations or forwarded carousels from other creators, for example Megan Anna Neff / Neurodivergent Insights, Lindsey Mackereth, neurodivergent_lou, Embrace Autism, and the channels Аутичные переводы, Нейр∞тлично and почему бы не попробовать. Nastya adds her own commentary, personal essays (her inner critic, unmasking, therapy, LDN) and designed cards. Her tone is warm and affirming and speaks as "we". She uses community vocabulary: маскинг, нейроотличный, выгорание, стимминг, identity-first language.

## Perspective and stance

The channel follows the neurodiversity paradigm: autism is not a pathology, functioning labels are rejected, self-diagnosis is accepted, and "you are not broken". It treats masking and life in a neurotypical world as the main drivers of burnout. It values rest, pacing and unmasking over pushing through, and it criticizes internalized capitalism and ableism. Its evidence is translated clinician and creator content, named research (Raymaker, Higgins, Hull, Milton), and lived experience. It also takes apart popular study claims, such as the paracetamol claim and the Z944 mouse "cure".

## Audience

Russian-speaking autistic, ADHD and AuDHD adults, often late-identified or self-identifying, facing burnout, sensory issues or chronic illness, plus their families and friends.

## How to use this knowledge

- Promotion and logistics were excluded, including Discord invitations and links.
