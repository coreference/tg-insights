# KNOWLEDGE INDEX — all channel knowledge bases

Single entry point for any agent answering from this library. It is deliberately short: it routes by **area** (cross-channel domain); the per-topic detail lives one level down.

## Protocol (follow in order)

1. **Start from the concept index** `index/concepts.md` (§3). Channels file the same subject under different areas (emotional regulation may sit in Mind & Emotions in one channel and in Rest & Burnout, Meditation or Leadership in another); every topic carries cross-channel **concept** tags, and the concept index lists, per concept, every topic of every channel with its area and scope. Pick the concept(s) matching the question and take **all** their topics.
2. **Safety net: search wide, in both languages.** Run `find` with the concept's synonyms plus the user's own words as separate queries, Russian **and** English — `python3 tg-archivist/archivist.py find --concept emotional-regulation "саморегуляция" "nervous system" "breathing"` (`--concept` adds every synonym of the concept and lifts the topics tagged with it). It searches every knowledge base, matches word forms and reports each hit's area. Then open the area indexes `index/areas/<area>.md` (§2) of the obvious area **and its neighbours**, including their *Also relevant* sections. Add every relevant hit, even in an unexpected area — never stop at the first channel or area that matches.
3. **Adopt each channel's skill before using its files.** For every channel you draw on, read its `agent-kb/SKILL.md` (listed in §1) and take on its persona and constraints *for that channel's material*: who is speaking, their stance and evidence type, attribution rules, caveats, what was excluded. The constraints of each channel stay attached to the insights taken from it.
4. **Read the selected knowledge files in full** (`<kb>/topics/<domain>/<topic>.md`). They are self-sufficient: complete insights and step-by-step methods with `[msg #id]` provenance. Do not answer from index lines.
5. **Synthesize across channels**: answer the question once, organised by idea — for each idea say which channel(s) hold it (`<channel> · <topic> [msg #id]`, adding the area when it is not the obvious one), where they agree, differ or complement each other. Keep every `(!)` caveat. If the library does not cover something, say so; do not fill the gap silently from general knowledge. **Surface the resources**: each topic file ends with *Resources and links* — give the user the relevant items (★ recommended ones first) with their link or file path: the files, guides, articles, studies and tools under *Resources*, and products, books, courses or pages under *External links*, labelled as such. All of them across channels: `index/resources.md`.
6. **Lighter processing** (overviews, skimming a whole area, many topics at once, or a tight context budget): read the NotebookLM study files instead — listed at the top of each area index, and gathered across channels in `index/notebooklm/<area>/`. Same content as the topic files, as plain text per domain with compact `[#id]` citations and no frontmatter or source lists. Switch to the topic files whenever exact provenance, dates, the routing fields or precise quotes matter.
7. **Verbatim originals** when asked: `<kb>/parsed/messages.jsonl` (text), `<kb>/parsed/media_text.jsonl` (text from images/PDFs), or the channel's `library.html`.

Paths are relative to the library root `/Users/coreference/projects/tg insights`; `<kb>` is a channel's folder, `sources/tg-channels/<channel>/` (§1 lists each), its original Telegram exports in `<kb>/raw-exports/`. Machine twin: `knowledge_index.json`. Concept index: `index/concepts.md` (+ `index/concepts.json`). Resources and links shared in the posts: `index/resources.md` (1690 resources, 1118 external links). Frameworks A–Z: `index/frameworks.md`. How the whole setup works: `TG_INSIGHTS_GUIDE.md`.

### На балансе с Катей Стрельниковой  (`na-balanse`)

«На балансе» (*On Balance*) is a Russian-language Telegram broadcast channel that calls itself a research channel about the search for energy, balance and self. It covers personal energy and how to manage it, rest and burnout, sleep, nutrition, movement, body image, emotions and inner work, behavior change and coaching (including a «dictionary of change» of look-alike concepts), impermanence and uncertainty, weight and eating-disorder recovery, and a critical look at the wellness industry. Posts run from December 2023 to mid-November 2026 (the knowledge base was extended with a second export, 2026-11-17). It was founded by two certified health coaches and, from October 2025, is the channel of health coach Katya Strelnikova.

### deep mind 2.0  (`deep-mind-2-0`)

"deep mind 2.0" is a Russian-language Telegram channel (posts from 10 November 2019 to 16 July 2026) by the founder of Deep Mind (launched as Deep Mind Consulting), a company working on mindfulness, wellbeing and corporate culture. Over the years it moves from conscious business and culture change toward meditation, trauma and the nervous system, burnout, mindful leadership, Theory U, vertical development, collective trauma and war, emigration, and the human side of AI.
